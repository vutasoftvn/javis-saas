# ADR-EXECUTIVE-BOARD-001: Governed Project Advisory Board & Deliberation Ledger

## Status
ACCEPTED 2026-09-11 (Implemented and verified across contracts, Company service, Agent Platform, and Flutter Hologram Hub).

## Context
Founders running early-stage startups and expansion ventures require multi-perspective strategic counsel (finance, marketing, technology, product, legal, compliance, talent) without the operational overhead and security risk of autonomous high-privilege agents. Previous prototypes suffered from three architectural failure modes:
1. **Title-as-Authority Ambiguity:** Agents designated as "CFO" or "CMO" attempting or assuming effectful operational capabilities without strict human authorization.
2. **Draft Contamination:** LLM agents observing peer deliberations and converging prematurely (groupthink or hallucination cascades) rather than providing independent orthogonal analysis.
3. **Workspace-Level Monoliths:** Advisory contexts defined at whole-workspace levels rather than bounded to specific Projects, causing cross-project data leakage.

## Decisions

### 1. Code-Owned Catalog & Multi-Language Generation
- The canonical catalog of 13 executive advisor roles (`ceo`, `cfo`, `cto`, `cmo`, `cpo`, `coo`, `cro`, `ciso`, `clo`, `cdo`, `chro`, `caio`, `chief_of_staff`) is defined in `shared/contracts/executive-advisor-roles.json`.
- The catalog is code-owned and immutable at runtime.
- Multi-language contract generators compile the definitions into TypeScript (`services/company/shared/contracts/executive-advisor-roles.generated.ts`), Python (`packages/agent/executive_board/models.py`), and Dart (`frontend/lib/modules/hologram_hub/models/executive_advisory_board.dart`).
- Strict validation (`make contracts-check` and `make skillpacks-validate`) ensures zero contract drift.

### 2. Founder-Only Authority (`role_id == 'founder'`)
- All board configurations, preset selections, role activations/disablements, deliberation framing, and decision finalizations require authenticated human Founder credentials (`role_id == 'founder'`).
- AI agents, workspace admins, and regular members are strictly prohibited from toggling roles, initiating deliberations, or deciding proposals.

### 3. Advisory L1 Boundary: Read & Propose Only
- Advisory roles operate strictly at capability level `advisory_l1`.
- A title conveys zero operational authority or effectful tools:
  - `cfo` can analyze financial statements and propose capital allocations, but CANNOT execute disbursements or sign contracts.
  - `cmo` can recommend marketing tactics, but CANNOT deploy live ads or publish campaigns.
  - `ciso` can audit security postures and suggest mitigation, but CANNOT alter firewall rules.
- Proposals generated by advisors are flagged `NOT_EXECUTED` until separate, explicit human execution pathways occur.

### 4. Company Ownership & Deliberation Ledger
- The Company service is the sole authoritative owner of the deliberation state machine:
  `DRAFT` -> `FRAMED` -> `ANALYSIS_QUEUED` -> `ANALYZING` -> `SYNTHESIS_QUEUED` -> `CRITIC_REVIEW` -> `AWAITING_FOUNDER` -> `DECIDED` (or `CANCELLED` / `EXPIRED`).
- All state transitions use Compare-And-Swap (CAS) version checks (`version` and `expectedVersion`) and emit transactional outbox events (`EXECUTIVE_DELIBERATION_FRAMED_V1`, `EXECUTIVE_ANALYSIS_COMPLETED_V1`, etc.).
- Deliberation frames snapshot pinned role specifications (`specId`, `specVersion`, `specHash`, `skillPins`) at framing time.

### 5. Isolated Analysis Without Peer Drafts
- The Agent Platform runs each advisor in isolation (`ExecutiveBoardRunner.run_advisor_analysis`).
- Pinned context includes only the project metadata, the deliberation question, designated evidence sources, and the role's skillpack.
- Advisors do NOT receive peer drafts during their analysis phase, preventing cross-agent contamination and groupthink.

### 6. No Auto-Progression to Decision
- Deliberations entering `AWAITING_FOUNDER` require explicit Founder decision actions (`APPROVE`, `MODIFY`, `REJECT`, `TABLE`).
- No timeout or autonomous worker rule can advance a deliberation to `DECIDED`.

## Consequences

### Positive
- Strict security isolation: compromised advisor runs cannot inflict destructive or unauthorized state mutations.
- Reproducibility and auditability: every advisor contribution is immutably appended to `project_executive_deliberation_analyses` with cryptographic hashes.
- Honest UI: Flutter Hologram Hub renders exact server state without optimistic fabrications or legacy mocks.

### Negative / Trade-offs
- Roles requiring unassigned startup profiles (such as `chief_of_staff` requiring an `operations` profile) remain in `UNAVAILABLE` state until suitable profiles exist.
- Human-in-the-loop latency: decisions require active Founder participation.
