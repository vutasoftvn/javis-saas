## SLIDE-BY-SLIDE SPECIFICATIONS

### Slide 1: Title & Core Thesis (Hero Presentation)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-01 — Takeaway Claim`
- **Layout & Composition**: Hero layout with bold 12-week calendar graphic on #070C18 canvas.
- **Header Badge**: `COSA ACADEMY · MODULE 00 · LESSON 0.6`
- **Main Headline**: **Your First 12-Week Year: Eliminating Annual Drift**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Why 12-month planning fails early-stage ventures and how 12-week cycles create unstoppable execution momentum.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Annual planning creates complacency: founders believe they have 10 months to recover from slow quarters.
  - A 12-Week Year compresses an entire year into 12 weeks: each week represents a month, each day represents a week.
  - The operating cycle creates extreme focus around one strategic theme and one or two measurable outcomes.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: THE 12-WEEK PRINCIPLE: Urgency is not panic; it is the deliberate compression of strategic milestones.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Visual compression: A long sprawling 12-month calendar bar compressing down into 12 dense, glowing weekly blocks.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Time compression graphic: A sprawling faint 12-month timeline collapsing into a focused, glowing teal 12-block sprint matrix on dark navy.*

### Slide 2: The Architecture of a 12-Week Cycle (Framework & Model)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-04 — Focus Framework`
- **Layout & Composition**: 3-tier pyramid structure connecting Theme, Outcome, and Weekly Tactics.
- **Header Badge**: `CYCLE ARCHITECTURE`
- **Main Headline**: **The 3 Levels of 12-Week Alignment**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: How strategic intent cascades into daily operational discipline.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Level 1: The Strategic Theme — A rallying cry focusing all company energy (e.g., 'First 10 Paying Customers').
  - Level 2: The Core Outcomes (1-2 Max) — Quantifiable commercial results that prove the theme was achieved.
  - Level 3: Weekly Execution Tactics — Non-negotiable weekly activities that directly drive the outcome (e.g., '15 buyer outreach calls/week').
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: ALIGNMENT RULE: If a weekly task cannot be traced directly to the cycle outcome, it does not belong in the plan.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Three-level pyramid diagram with glowing edges and connecting directional arrows.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Clean tiered triangular framework on dark canvas, top peak glowing gold for Theme, middle tier glowing cyan for Outcomes, foundation glowing teal for Tactics.*

### Slide 3: The Weekly Operating Rhythm (Operating Workflow)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-03 — Operating Loop`
- **Layout & Composition**: Monday-to-Friday horizontal rhythm pipeline.
- **Header Badge**: `WEEKLY CADENCE`
- **Main Headline**: **The Rhythm of Accountability**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: How a high-performing founder runs the week from Monday morning to Friday evening.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Monday Planning (60 mins): Review cycle score, identify the week's top 3 non-negotiables, commit to teammates/advisors.
  - Midweek Execution (Tue-Thu): Protect deep-work blocks, address blockers in Hologram Hub, coordinate agent tasks.
  - Friday Review (45 mins): Score execution percentage (target >85%), log empirical learnings, celebrate wins, prepare next week.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: ACCOUNTABILITY SCORE: Execution percentage matters more than immediate results. Consistency produces breakthroughs.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Horizontal 5-day tracker with Monday kickoff node, Midweek execution pulse, and Friday scoring flag.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Timeline diagram showing Monday planning beacon, steady midweek pulse lines, and glowing Friday retrospective checkpoint on deep navy.*

### Slide 4: Adaptation Rules & Mid-Cycle Course Correction (Decision Framework)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-06 — Decision Checkpoint`
- **Layout & Composition**: Flowchart with branch conditions for mid-cycle pivots.
- **Header Badge**: `CYCLE ADJUSTMENTS`
- **Main Headline**: **When to Hold vs. When to Pivot Mid-Cycle**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Balancing execution perseverance with the necessity of acknowledging fatal assumptions.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Rule 1: Never change tactics in the first 3 weeks due to emotional discomfort or slow starts.
  - Rule 2: If Week 6 arrives and execution score is >85% but leading indicators show zero response, trigger a Strategic Review.
  - Rule 3: You may adjust the *tactics* to reach the outcome, but keep the *theme* stable unless catastrophic evidence emerges.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: DECISION GATE: Distinguish between execution failure (didn't do the work) and hypothesis failure (did the work, customer rejected it).
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Decision tree flowchart showing evaluation branch at Week 6 with Keep/Modify/Abort pathways.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Flowchart on dark background with Week 6 checkpoint split into teal 'Refine Tactic' and rose 'Pivot Bet' pathways.*

### Slide 5: Anti-Patterns vs. Best Practices (Comparative Matrix)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-02 — Definition Contrast`
- **Layout & Composition**: Side-by-side comparison table.
- **Header Badge**: `EXECUTION PITFALLS`
- **Main Headline**: **Planning Theater vs. 12-Week Mastery**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Avoiding the common failure modes of productivity systems.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Trap: Committing to 7 different strategic goals → Result: Fragmented effort and zero goals achieved.
  - Trap: Focusing on lag metrics you can't control (e.g., $100k revenue) instead of lead metrics (e.g., 20 demo calls).
  - Best Practice: Commit to 1 primary outcome and relentlessly track weekly execution input discipline.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: DISCIPLINE: You cannot control whether the customer signs today. You can control how many qualified proposals you send.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Comparison matrix highlighting over-commitment traps vs. sharp singular focus.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Table layout on dark canvas: red warning badges for multi-tasking sprawl; teal checkmarks for singular lead-metric focus.*

### Slide 6: Founder Action Checkpoint (Action Deliverable)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-07 — Learner Action`
- **Layout & Composition**: Action template container card.
- **Header Badge**: `EXERCISE: 12-WEEK BLUEPRINT`
- **Main Headline**: **Draft Your 12-Week Year in COSA**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Set up your upcoming 12-week cycle in the COSA 12-Week Year workspace.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Step 1: Define your 12-Week Strategic Theme in 5 words or less.
  - Step 2: Set exactly ONE primary outcome with a quantifiable target number.
  - Step 3: Write down your 3 non-negotiable execution commitments for Week 1.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: CYCLE COMMITMENT: 'For the next 12 weeks, our single priority is [Theme], measured by achieving [Specific Outcome].'
