- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-03 — Operating Loop`
- **Layout & Composition**: 6-stage horizontal accordion matrix with question headers.
- **Header Badge**: `LIFECYCLE PHASES`
- **Main Headline**: **The 6 Core Lifecycle Questions**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Every stage answers exactly one existential question before capital is scaled.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - P0 (Discovery): Is the customer problem real, urgent, and pervasive?
  - P1 (Solution Fit): Does our proposed prototype create meaningful, measurable relief?
  - P2 (Business Model): Are customers willing to pay sustainable prices to use it?
  - P3 (Controlled Pilot): Can we deliver value reliably to a cohort of real beta users?
  - P4 (PMF & Growth): Does customer retention repeat without artificial founder subsidies?
  - P5/P6 (Scale & Governance): Can the organization deliver unit-economic profitability at volume?
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: GATE REQUIREMENT: Never build a full product (P1) for an unvalidated problem (P0). Never scale marketing (P4) without retention (P3).

- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-06 — Decision Checkpoint`
- **Layout & Composition**: Two-panel split comparing Synthetic Milestones vs. Real Evidence Gates.
- **Header Badge**: `GOVERNANCE ISOLATION`
- **Main Headline**: **Course Completion vs. Stage Transition**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Why reading curriculum or passing quizzes never qualifies a venture for stage advancement.

### Slide 4: The Danger of Premature Scaling (Risk & Diagnostics)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-04 — Focus Framework`
- **Layout & Composition**: 3-column failure mode breakdown with danger callouts.
- **Header Badge**: `FAILURE ARCHAEOLOGY`
- **Main Headline**: **What Happens When You Scale Out of Sequence?**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Analyzing the three catastrophic traps of premature advancement.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Skipping P0 (No real problem): Building an elegant, bug-free solution that nobody wants or downloads.
  - Skipping P2 (No pricing validation): Acquiring thousands of free users who churn the moment you introduce a subscription paywall.
  - Skipping P3 (No pilot retention): Pouring marketing dollars into a 'leaky bucket' where customer churn destroys cash reserves.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: FINANCIAL WARNING: Scaling marketing on a product that doesn't retain accelerates bankruptcy, not success.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Three red danger containers (#F43F5E) illustrating common structural failure modes.

### Slide 5: Anti-Patterns vs. Best Practices (Comparative Matrix)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-02 — Definition Contrast`
- **Layout & Composition**: Side-by-side comparison table.
- **Header Badge**: `STAGE DISCIPLINE`
- **Main Headline**: **Founder Impatience vs. Empirical Stage Gates**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: How experienced founders navigate the lifecycle stages.
- **Core Slide Content**:

  - Trap: Hiring salespeople before the founder closes 10 deals vs. Practice: Mastering the founder-led sales motion first.
  - Trap: Rationalizing negative customer signals as 'user error' vs. Practice: Welcoming negative data as runway-saving truth.

- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Comparison matrix with rose penalty badges and teal validation checks.
