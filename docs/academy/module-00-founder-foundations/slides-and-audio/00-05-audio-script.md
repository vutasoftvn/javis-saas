### [SLIDE 1 AUDIO] — Title & Core Thesis (25s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 1 (Slide 1: Linear progression of six glowing stage-gate pillars.)
**Tone**: *Authoritative, cautionary, experienced.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "Why do most promising startups fail? [pause 0.5s] Venture researchers have studied thousands of companies, and the number one killer is always the same: **premature scaling**."
>
> "Founders spend money hiring people and buying ads before they have proven that customers truly need what they built. [pause 0.5s] In COSA, your progress is governed by a strict lifecycle: you learn before you build, and you validate before you scale."
>

### [SLIDE 2 AUDIO] — The Six Existential Questions (30s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 2 (Slide 2: Six vertical cards showing P0 through P5 lifecycle questions.)
**Tone**: *Structured, systematic.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "The COSA lifecycle breaks venture creation into six sequential questions. [pause 0.5s] In P0 Discovery: is the problem real? In P1 Solution Fit: does your prototype solve it? In P2 Business Model: will customers pay?"
>
> "In P3 Pilot: can you deliver it reliably? In P4 Growth: does demand repeat? And in P5 Scale: can you operate profitably? [pause 0.5s] You cannot skip a step; every skipped question turns into a fatal trap later."
>

### [SLIDE 3 AUDIO] — Evidence-Based Progression (25s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 3 (Slide 3: Firewall isolating academic learning from live production evidence.)
**Tone**: *Direct, uncompromising.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "Here is a critical rule in COSA: **learning completion is not venture evidence**. [pause 0.5s] You can finish every lesson in this academy, pass every simulation, and earn high scores."
>
> "That proves your intellectual understanding, but it does not advance your project stage. [pause 0.5s] Only verified, real-world customer artifacts—like signed contracts and usage telemetry—can unlock stage advancement."
>

### [SLIDE 4 AUDIO] — The Danger of Premature Scaling (25s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 4 (Slide 4: Three red hazard cards depicting premature scaling traps.)
**Tone**: *Serious, diagnostic.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "Consider what happens when you jump ahead. [pause 0.5s] If you skip problem validation, you build flawless software that nobody needs. If you skip pricing validation, you build a user base that vanishes the moment you ask for money."
>
> "And if you scale marketing before you have retention, you are pouring expensive fuel into a broken engine. [pause 0.5s] Respect the sequence."
>

### [SLIDE 5 AUDIO] — Anti-Patterns vs. Best Practices (25s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 5 (Slide 5: Contrast table showing founder impatience versus stage discipline.)
**Tone**: *Grounded, mentoring.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "Great founders resist the temptation of premature public fanfare. [pause 0.5s] Do not hire a sales team before the founder understands how to sell the product manually."
>
> "Do not celebrate thousands of signups if those users never return the second week. [pause 0.5s] Embrace negative feedback early when changes are cheap and quick to make."
>

### [SLIDE 6 AUDIO] — Founder Action Checkpoint (25s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 6 (Slide 6: Stage diagnostic exercise card with slider pin.)
**Tone**: *Action-oriented, inspiring.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "Now, locate your venture's true position. [pause 0.5s] Be brutally honest with yourself. Are you in Discovery, Solution Fit, or Business Model validation?"
>
> "Identify the single biggest unanswered question facing your project today. [pause 0.5s] Define the exact evidence needed to answer it, and focus your entire team's energy there."
>
