### Slide 2: The Objective vs. Key Result Anatomy (Framework & Model)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-02 — Definition Contrast`
- **Layout & Composition**: Two-card comparative breakdown detailing Objective vs. Key Result properties.
- **Header Badge**: `CONCEPT ANATOMY`
- **Main Headline**: **Qualitative Inspiration vs. Quantitative Proof**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Deconstructing how Objectives and Key Results complement each other.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - The Objective (Where are we going?): Qualitative, ambitious, memorable, and motivating (e.g., 'Achieve unquestionable product love among beta designers').
  - Key Result 1: Quantitative metric tracking customer behavior (e.g., 'Weekly retention rate increases from 25% to 60%').
  - Key Result 2: Quantitative metric tracking customer commitment (e.g., '10 beta teams migrate their production workflow to COSA').
  - Key Result 3: Quantitative metric tracking quality/satisfaction (e.g., 'NPS score reaches +50 with 15 verified qualitative quotes').
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: KEY RESULT LITMUS TEST: Can a neutral third party look at your database and verify whether you hit the number without debate?
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Split card: Left card showing glowing teal Objective header; right card displaying three bulleted quantitative metrics.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Comparative layout on dark canvas: Left card with glowing typography for qualitative goal; right card with digital gauge meters showing numbers.*

### Slide 3: The OKR-to-Execution Cascade (Architectural Cascade)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-04 — Focus Framework`
- **Layout & Composition**: Top-to-bottom cascading flowchart connecting Vision to Daily Tasks.
- **Header Badge**: `SYSTEM ALIGNMENT`
- **Main Headline**: **Connecting Strategy to the Daily Task List**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: How OKRs bridge long-term vision with Monday morning execution.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Strategic Vision: The multi-year destination of the venture.
  - Project Bet: The bounded 12-week commercial initiative.
  - Objective (OKR): The qualitative direction for this cycle.
  - Key Results (OKR): The quantifiable proof points.
  - Weekly Tasks: The specific activities executed in the current sprint to move those Key Results.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: TRACEABILITY: Every task in your COSA board should trace directly back to an active Key Result.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Cascading waterfall visual with translucent cards connecting Vision → Project → OKR → Weekly Tasks.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Flowing vertical cascade diagram on dark navy #070C18, neon teal connecting conduits linking strategic containers down to task cards.*

### Slide 4: Common Early-Stage OKR Mistakes (Risk & Diagnostics)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-06 — Decision Checkpoint`
- **Layout & Composition**: 3-column grid highlighting the top 3 OKR blunders.
- **Header Badge**: `OKR ANTI-PATTERNS`
- **Main Headline**: **The Three OKR Traps That Paralyze Founders**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Avoid turning a focus tool into an administrative nightmare.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Trap 1: The 'Task-as-Key-Result' Blunder — Writing 'Launch new landing page' instead of 'Acquire 100 qualified trial leads'. (Tasks are inputs, not outcomes).
  - Trap 2: The Goal Overload Trap — Setting 5 Objectives with 20 Key Results. (When everything is a priority, nothing is).
  - Trap 3: Set-and-Forget — Writing OKRs at the start of the quarter and never reviewing them until the quarter ends.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: GOLDEN RULE: A Key Result must measure an outcome or customer reaction, never a completed to-do item.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Three dark containers with red warning banners detailing common mistakes.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Three dark containers with crimson hazard accents, minimal wireframe icons showing task list disguised as metric, overloaded balance scale, and dusty drawer.*

### Slide 5: Anti-Patterns vs. Best Practices (Comparative Matrix)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-02 — Definition Contrast`
- **Layout & Composition**: Side-by-side comparison table.
- **Header Badge**: `DISCIPLINED GOAL-SETTING`
- **Main Headline**: **Corporate Rituals vs. Lean Founder Focus**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Adapting OKRs to the reality of early-stage discovery.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Corporate Practice: Rigid 12-month goal locks with tied compensation vs. Founder Practice: Flexible learning targets tied to survival.
  - Corporate Practice: Cascading 5 layers of department OKRs vs. Founder Practice: One shared company OKR for the entire founding team.
  - Corporate Practice: Punishing missed aspirational targets vs. Founder Practice: Treating missed targets as diagnostic evidence.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: DIAGNOSTIC MINDSET: If you miss a Key Result, don't hide it. Ask: 'What assumption did we get wrong?'
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Table comparing traditional corporate OKR practices with lean startup OKR methods.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Comparison table on dark canvas: muted grey icons for corporate bureaucracy; glowing cyan icons for agile startup focus.*

### Slide 6: Founder Action Checkpoint (Action Deliverable)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-07 — Learner Action`
- **Layout & Composition**: Action template card container.
- **Header Badge**: `EXERCISE: OKR DRAFT`
- **Main Headline**: **Draft Your Lean OKR Set in COSA**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Open the OKR view in COSA Strategy and write your single company objective for this cycle.

  - Step 1: Write ONE qualitative Objective that captures your primary 12-week challenge.
  - Step 2: Draft 2 to 3 measurable Key Results with clear baseline numbers and target numbers.
  - Step 3: Connect your OKR directly to your primary active Project Bet.
