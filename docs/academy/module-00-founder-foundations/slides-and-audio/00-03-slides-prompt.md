- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-02 — Definition Contrast`
- **Layout & Composition**: Two distinct containers contrasting Founder-Owned vs. AI-Assisted domains.
- **Header Badge**: `RESPONSIBILITY MAPPING`
- **Main Headline**: **Founder-Owned Decisions vs. AI-Assisted Execution**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Drawing clear operational lines to prevent catastrophic governance failures.

- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: A sustainable weekly rhythm designed for single-operator scalability.

  - 2. Define the Target Outcome: Establish verifiable success criteria before starting.

### Slide 5: Anti-Patterns vs. Best Practices (Comparative Matrix)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-06 — Decision Checkpoint`
- **Layout & Composition**: Comparison table contrasting burnout traps with leveraged mastery.
- **Header Badge**: `SURVIVAL DISCIPLINE`
- **Main Headline**: **Solo Traps vs. Leveraged Founder Practices**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Avoiding the two deadly extremes: manual burnout vs. unmonitored automation.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Trap: Doing 100% of data entry and admin tasks manually → Result: Founder exhaustion and stalled growth.
  - Trap: Blindly trusting AI output without verification → Result: Damaged customer relationships and hallucinated strategy.
  - Best Practice: Use AI for 80% draft acceleration; invest your human brain in the critical 20% judgment.

- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Table highlighting red hazard flags vs. teal operational guardrails.
