- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-02 — Definition Contrast`
- **Layout & Composition**: 4-tier cascading hierarchy diagram from Workspace down to Evidence.
- **Header Badge**: `COSA DATA MODEL`
- **Main Headline**: **Workspace vs. Project vs. Task vs. Evidence**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Understanding how commercial intent translates into daily action without confusion.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Workspace: The entire company container holding all assets, legal entities, and strategy.
  - Project: A bounded commercial bet testing a specific hypothesis (e.g., 'Validate B2B SaaS for Logistics').
  - Task: A concrete, owner-assigned unit of execution required to generate proof.
  - Evidence: Empirical, verifiable facts gathered from customer interactions that prove or disprove the hypothesis.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: DATA INTEGRITY: Tasks produce outputs; projects produce validated evidence.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Cascading modular diagram showing parent-child relationships between Workspace, Project, Task, and Evidence.

### Slide 3: The Anatomy of a Project Brief (Framework & Specification)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-05 — Example Artifact`
- **Layout & Composition**: Structured document card preview showing the 5 essential project fields.
- **Header Badge**: `PROJECT BRIEF BLUEPRINT`
- **Main Headline**: **The 5 Core Elements of a Rigorous Project Brief**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Every new initiative must articulate these five parameters before work begins.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - 1. Target Customer Segment: Who experiences the pain with highest urgency?
  - 2. Problem Statement: What observable breakdown or friction are they suffering today?
  - 3. Core Solution Hypothesis: What mechanism will solve this friction?
  - 4. Target Outcome: What metric or condition proves the hypothesis true?
  - 5. Stopping Rule: What threshold of negative evidence will cause us to abort or pivot?
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: ESSENTIAL SAFEGUARD: If you don't define your stopping rule upfront, you will waste months rationalizing failure.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Document artifact container on surface #0D172A displaying five numbered parameter badges.

### Slide 4: Boundary Enforcement (Operational Governance)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-04 — Focus Framework`
- **Layout & Composition**: In-Scope vs. Out-of-Scope boundary diagram.
- **Header Badge**: `SCOPE DISCIPLINE`
- **Main Headline**: **Protecting the Project Boundary**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Saying 'no' to 90% of ideas to ensure the 10% that matter actually finish.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Included (In Scope): Minimal tasks strictly required to validate the core assumption.
  - Excluded (Out of Scope): Edge case features, multi-platform support, premature visual redesigns, automated billing.
  - The Parking Lot: Storing tangential ideas in the Project Vault without letting them derail the current sprint.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: LEADERSHIP RULE: A project is defined more by what you refuse to build than what you include.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Visual perimeter ring: Green glowing inner core for In-Scope tasks, red exterior ring deflecting distracting tasks.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Perimeter defense diagram: Bright teal circular boundary shielding core priority tokens from deflected red distraction vectors.*

### Slide 5: Anti-Patterns vs. Best Practices (Comparative Matrix)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-06 — Decision Checkpoint`
- **Layout & Composition**: Side-by-side comparison table.
- **Header Badge**: `PROJECT PITFALLS`
- **Main Headline**: **Endless Tinkering vs. Bounded Execution**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Identifying why early initiatives fail to reach completion.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Trap: Open-ended research without a deadline → Result: Analysis paralysis and zero customer contact.
  - Trap: The Never-Ending MVP that keeps adding features → Result: Burned runway and unvalidated code.
  - Best Practice: Timebox the initiative to 2 to 4 weeks with a binary Go/No-Go decision gate at the end.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: DECISION RULE: If a task does not directly generate evidence for the project brief, archive it immediately.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Table contrasting endless loops with crisp timeboxed milestones.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Comparative graphic: Left shows infinite spinning red loop; right shows crisp teal timeline with finish line flag.*

### Slide 6: Founder Action Checkpoint (Action Deliverable)
