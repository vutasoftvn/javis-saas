# Lesson 0.1 — Welcome to COSA: Your Founder Operating System

**Target duration:** 3 × 28 seconds = approximately 84 seconds

**Learner promise:** The learner understands COSA as one connected operating system and knows where to begin.

| Video | Narrative job and composition | In-frame teaching slide | Prompt |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Hook: founders do not lack tools; they lack a way to focus attention and decisions. Founder focus, no slide. | None | [01](prompts/01-lesson-00-01-video-01.md) |
| 02 | Model: COSA connects Project, direction, weekly rhythm, work and human-owned decisions. Founder + slide. | `Project → Direction → Rhythm → Action` | [02](prompts/02-lesson-00-01-video-02.md) |
| 03 | Application: explain the learning journey and choose a starting path. Founder + action slide. | `Learn → Project → Validate → Execute → Review` | [03](prompts/03-lesson-00-01-video-03.md) |

**End action:** Choose one path: “Tôi có một ý tưởng”, “Tôi đang kiểm chứng”, “Tôi đã có khách hàng”, or “Tôi đang vận hành.”
