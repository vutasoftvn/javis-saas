# Lesson 0.2 — What Is a Startup?

**Target duration:** 3 × 28 seconds = approximately 84 seconds

**Learner promise:** The learner can distinguish a startup from an idea, a project, and a traditional small business.

| Video | Narrative job and composition | In-frame teaching slide | Prompt |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Hook: startup is not an app, fundraising, or pure hustle. Founder focus, no slide. | None | [04](prompts/04-lesson-00-02-video-01.md) |
| 02 | Definition: startup is a search under uncertainty; distinguish idea, project, startup, and small business. Founder + slide. | `A search under uncertainty` | [05](prompts/05-lesson-00-02-video-02.md) |
| 03 | Application: reduce uncertainty through evidence, then write a testable belief. Founder + action slide. | `Assumption → Evidence → Decision` | [06](prompts/06-lesson-00-02-video-03.md) |

**End action:** Write: “We believe that [customer] struggles with [problem] in [context].”
