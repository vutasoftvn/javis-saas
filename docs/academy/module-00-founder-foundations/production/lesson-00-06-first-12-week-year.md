# Lesson 0.6 — Your First 12-Week Year

**Target duration:** 3 × 28 seconds = approximately 84 seconds

**Learner promise:** The learner can decide whether a 12-week cycle is appropriate and turn one direction into weekly commitments.

| Video | Narrative job and composition | In-frame teaching slide | Prompt |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Hook: 12 weeks creates focus, not a promise of startup success; uncertainty may require a shorter cycle. Founder focus, no slide. | None | [16](prompts/16-lesson-00-06-video-01.md) |
| 02 | Model: explain Theme, Outcome, Weekly Commitments, and Review. Founder + focus framework. | `Theme → Outcome → Weekly commitments → Review` | [17](prompts/17-lesson-00-06-video-02.md) |
| 03 | Application: choose three week-one actions, then review learning and adjust. Founder + example/action slide. | `Three actions for week one` | [18](prompts/18-lesson-00-06-video-03.md) |

**End action:** Draft one theme, one outcome, and three week-one actions; use a two- or four-week loop if the project is still highly uncertain.
