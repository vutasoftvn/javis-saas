# Module 0 — Seedance Production Pack

## Production decision

Each Academy lesson is assembled from **three complete Seedance videos**, not from separate presenter and slide assets. Every generated video is a finished scene: the same illustrated founder is present, speaks English, and appears beside a teaching slide whenever a slide helps the learner understand the idea.

This gives every lesson a simple learning rhythm:

1. **Hook** — founder-led; no slide; the founder is larger in frame.
2. **Model or example** — founder beside one simple teaching slide.
3. **Application** — founder beside one action or example slide; ends with the learner's next step.

At 28 seconds per clip, each lesson is approximately **84 seconds** after the three clips are joined. There are no generated product screens, no detached presenter shots, and no detached slide shots.

## Host character bible

Use one recurring illustrated host in every clip:

- **Role:** Vietnamese founder and operating coach, male, approximately 40 years old.
- **Visual style:** polished editorial illustration; warm, modern, intelligent, credible. Avoid childish cartoon styling, exaggerated anime proportions, or photorealism.
- **Appearance:** short dark hair, calm approachable expression, smart-casual dark overshirt or blazer over a neutral shirt, with restrained COSA teal and sky-blue accents.
- **Performance:** direct eye contact, measured hand gestures, confident but not salesy. He teaches as a fellow founder, never as a corporate trainer.
- **Continuity:** create Video 01 first. For Videos 02–24, reuse a still from the best frame of Video 01 as the character reference if Seedance supports a reference image. Keep the same face, haircut, wardrobe, palette, studio and camera language.

## Frame language

### Founder focus — no slide

Use this only for a hook, reflection, or transition where an explanatory slide would add noise.

- Founder occupies about **65–70%** of the frame in a medium-close shot.
- Background: deep navy founder studio with a soft teal/sky glow, subtle planning-board texture or abstract light lines; never a product UI.
- Use one short kinetic keyword only if useful. Keep it behind or beside the founder, never as a slide screen.
- Camera uses a very slow push-in or subtle parallax so the founder feels present and human.

### Founder + teaching slide

Use this for a definition, framework, example, or action.

- Founder stands on the **left 35–40%** of a 16:9 frame; the teaching slide is on a physical-looking, borderless floating screen on the **right 60–65%**.
- The slide has one takeaway title, one visual model, and at most three supporting labels. It is a conceptual slide, never app UI, dashboard, buttons, tabs, or controls.
- Founder can glance and gesture toward the screen while keeping a calm explanatory stance. The screen softly illuminates the founder's right side.

## Audio and edit language

Every individual Seedance prompt contains its own audio instruction so it can be created independently.

- **Voice:** native-level English male, about 40, warm and steady, neutral international accent, conversational pacing. The spoken script is written inside each prompt and should be followed exactly.
- **Music:** one continuous family of minimal warm electronic-ambient music, about 76–82 BPM, no vocals and no aggressive beat. Voice remains clearly dominant; music sits low underneath.
- **Sound design:** use only restrained soft teal-light whooshes, a gentle tonal swell when a slide appears, and a small resolve tone at the action prompt. No notification pings, keyboard typing, crowd noise, applause, or cinematic impacts.
- **Transitions:** retain a 0.75-second visual handle at the beginning and end of each clip. Join clips with a 0.4–0.6-second soft dissolve or a teal light sweep. Never use a hard cut, glitch, whip-pan, or an unrelated change of studio.
- **Captions:** keep a safe lower-third area for later English captions. Do not place important slide content in the lowest 18% of the frame.

## Design basis from COSA

The teaching slides use the visual language in the actual COSA codebase: deep canvas `#070C18`, deep navy glow `#0B1934`, surface `#0D172A`, teal `#14B8A6`, light teal `#2DD4BF`, sky blue `#38BDF8`, rose warning `#F43F5E`, white, and muted slate `#94A3B8`. See [slide-system.md](slide-system.md) for the complete in-frame slide system.
