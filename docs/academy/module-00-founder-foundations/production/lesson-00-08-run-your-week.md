# Lesson 0.8 — Run Your Week: Tasks, Decisions, Approvals, and AI Workforce

**Target duration:** 3 × 28 seconds = approximately 84 seconds

**Learner promise:** The learner can run a weekly operating rhythm based on outcomes, decisions, and responsible AI support.

| Video | Narrative job and composition | In-frame teaching slide | Prompt |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Hook: do not let notifications define the week; begin with one outcome and the Top 3. Founder focus, no slide. | None | [22](prompts/22-lesson-00-08-video-01.md) |
| 02 | Model: distinguish tasks, repeatable workflows, decisions, and approval checkpoints. Founder + operating model. | `Task ≠ Decision` | [23](prompts/23-lesson-00-08-video-02.md) |
| 03 | Application: delegate work to AI while retaining accountability; finish the week by reviewing blockers and decisions. Founder + action slide. | `Plan → Execute → Review → Adjust` | [24](prompts/24-lesson-00-08-video-03.md) |

**End action:** Create three weekly tasks, one decision record, one approval boundary, and one AI-assisted activity.
