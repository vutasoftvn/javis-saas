# Module 5 — Seedance Production Pack

Module 5 has nine lessons, each made from three complete **28-second English** founder-led Seedance videos: (1) founder hook without slide, (2) founder plus teaching model, and (3) founder plus action or decision slide. Join the three clips into an approximately 84-second lesson with a soft dissolve or teal-light sweep.

Keep the approved illustrated Vietnamese male founder, about 40, from Modules 0–4: same face, wardrobe, English voice, 16:9 framing and camera language. Use a COSA deep-navy **growth studio** with subtle repeatable-growth paths and warm desk light; never create product UI, dashboards, CRM screens, charts, or detached presenter/slide assets.

Every prompt includes a native-level English male voice with neutral international accent, warm 78 BPM electronic ambient music, restrained sound effects, English caption safe area, and 0.75-second start/end handles. See Module 0’s [in-frame slide system](../../module-00-founder-foundations/production/slide-system.md) for the visual language.
