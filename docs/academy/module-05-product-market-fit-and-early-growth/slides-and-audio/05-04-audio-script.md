> "In Lesson 5.4, you will learn to **Optimize Acquisition Channels**. [pause 0.5s] You will look past top-of-funnel vanity clicks and evaluate channels by true customer quality, sales velocity, and six-month retention."

### [SLIDE 2 AUDIO] — The 4 Channel Scorecard Dimensions (30s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 2 (Slide 2: Four cards showing CAC, Velocity Days, LTV Multiplier, and Retention %.)
**Tone**: *Instructional, structured.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "Score every acquisition channel on four dimensions. [pause 0.5s] First, **Fully-Loaded CAC**—the total cost, including ad spend and founder hours, to win one buyer. Second, **Pipeline Velocity**—how many days it takes from first click to signed contract."
>
> "Third, **Customer Quality**—the average deal size and expansion potential. [pause 0.5s] And fourth, **Month-6 Retention Rate**. A channel that costs one hundred dollars to acquire a customer who cancels in two months is a disaster compared to an eight-hundred-dollar channel where customers stay for four years."
>

### [SLIDE 3 AUDIO] — Channel Reallocation in Practice (25s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 3 (Slide 3: Leaking red pipe vs. solid glowing gold conduit delivering bullion.)
**Tone**: *Strategic, decisive.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "Consider the classic capital allocation choice. [pause 0.5s] Paid social ads might produce fifty signups a month at a cheap cost, but eighty percent churn immediately. That is pouring expensive fuel into a leaky bucket."
>
> "Meanwhile, targeted outbound sales might cost five times more per lead, but eighty-five percent of them retain and expand. [pause 0.5s] Have the courage to kill the cheap vanity channel, and reallocate one hundred percent of your capital to the high-retention motion."
>

### [SLIDE 5 AUDIO] — Anti-Patterns vs. Best Practices (25s)
