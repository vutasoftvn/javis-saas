### Slide 2: The 5 Standard Pipeline Stages (Pipeline Architecture)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-04 — Focus Framework`
- **Layout & Composition**: 5-stage horizontal chevron pipeline on surface #0D172A.
- **Header Badge**: `PIPELINE ARCHITECTURE`
- **Main Headline**: **The 5 Stages of a High-Velocity B2B Pipeline**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Clear definitions and non-negotiable exit criteria for each deal stage in Sales CRM.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Stage 1: Inbound / Outbound Lead — Account matches beachhead criteria; initial contact initiated.
  - Stage 2: Discovery & Qualification (BANT Audit) — Verify Budget, Authority, Need, and Timeline. Uncover current workaround.
  - Stage 3: Solution Demo & Business Case — 30-min live walkthrough tailored strictly to their validated problem. Present pilot ROI numbers.
  - Stage 4: Executive Proposal & Security Review — Formal commercial quote sent to Economic Buyer; IT security checklist provided.
  - Stage 5: Closed-Won / Handoff — Contract executed in Approvals; deal moves to Onboarding Workflow.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: STAGE INTEGRITY: A deal can never advance to Stage 3 (Demo) without confirming Budget and Need in Stage 2.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Five horizontal chevrons in progressive cyan to teal gradient with stage numbers and exit criteria badges.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Five sleek glass chevrons in horizontal row on dark canvas, progressive cyan glow, labeled Lead, Qualify, Demo, Proposal, Closed-Won.*

### Slide 3: Objection Battlecards: Handling the Big 3 (Tactical Battlecards)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-02 — Definition Contrast`
- **Layout & Composition**: 3-column container comparison: Price, Security, Status Quo.
- **Header Badge**: `OBJECTION BATTLECARDS`
- **Main Headline**: **The 3 Critical Sales Battlecards**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Standardized scripts for neutralizing the most common commercial objections.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Objection 1: 'Your price is too expensive.' → Script: Reframe against their current manual workaround: 'Our $6k annual fee replaces $40k in wasted payroll.'
  - Objection 2: 'We already use Excel / Google Sheets.' → Script: Acknowledge Excel's flexibility, then highlight operational risk: 'Excel doesn't have automated audit trails.'
  - Objection 3: 'We don't have time to implement a new tool.' → Script: Emphasize Time-to-First-Value: 'Our onboarding takes under 30 minutes with zero IT setup.'
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: OBJECTION RULE: Never argue with a prospect. Acknowledge their concern, reframe the trade-off, and cite a pilot case study.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Three sleek vertical cards with icons for Dollar, Shield, and Stopwatch, displaying objection text and verified counter-scripts.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Three modern UI battlecards on dark navy canvas, glowing gold icons, clear quote typography contrasting objections with winning responses.*

### Slide 5: Anti-Patterns vs. Best Practices (Comparative Matrix)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-06 — Decision Checkpoint`
- **Layout & Composition**: Side-by-side comparison table.
- **Header Badge**: `PLAYBOOK PITFALLS`
- **Main Headline**: **The 'Maverick Salesperson' vs. Process Discipline**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Why undisciplined sales reps destroy early-stage startups.
