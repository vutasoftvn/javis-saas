### [SLIDE 2 AUDIO] — NPS vs. CSAT Deconstructed (30s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 2 (Slide 2: Two cards showing quarterly NPS loyalty radar vs. instant CSAT rating stars.)
**Tone**: *Instructional, precise.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "Understand when to use each survey primitive. [pause 0.5s] **Net Promoter Score** measures long-term brand loyalty. You ask it quarterly: 'How likely are you to recommend us to a colleague?' Scores from nine to ten are Promoters; scores from zero to six are Detractors."
>
> "**CSAT**, on the other hand, is transactional. [pause 0.5s] You trigger it immediately after a customer finishes a support ticket or completes a data import. CSAT finds bugs in your user experience; NPS measures the overall health of your customer relationship."
>

### [SLIDE 3 AUDIO] — The Closed-Loop Feedback Protocol (25s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 3 (Slide 3: Three-branch flowchart: Crimson triage, Amber survey, Teal referral.)
**Tone**: *Action-oriented, practical.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "A feedback score requires immediate operational follow-through. [pause 0.5s] If a customer gives you a score of five, that is a Detractor. Trigger an automated task to call or email them within twenty-four hours to ask what broke."
>
> "If they give you a seven or eight, ask what single feature would make it a ten. [pause 0.5s] And if they give you a nine or ten, do not just smile! Immediately provide a referral link or ask them to write a review on G2 while their excitement is high."
>
