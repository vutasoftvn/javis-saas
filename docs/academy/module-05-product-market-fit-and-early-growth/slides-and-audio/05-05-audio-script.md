**Tone**: *Commercial, structured, authoritative.*

### [SLIDE 2 AUDIO] — The 5 Standard Pipeline Stages (30s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 2 (Slide 2: Five chevrons showing Lead, Qualify, Demo, Proposal, and Closed-Won.)
**Tone**: *Instructional, structured.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "Your pipeline in Sales CRM has five non-negotiable stages. [pause 0.5s] Lead: initial qualification against your beachhead. Qualification: the BANT audit—verifying budget, authority, need, and timeline before booking a demo."
>
> "Demo: a thirty-minute walkthrough tailored strictly to their validated pain. [pause 0.5s] Proposal: sending formal commercial terms to the economic buyer. And Closed-Won: signing the contract and handing off to onboarding. Never let a deal advance to a demo without qualifying budget first."
>

### [SLIDE 3 AUDIO] — Objection Battlecards: Handling the Big 3 (25s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 3 (Slide 3: Three battlecards showing Price, Excel Workaround, and Implementation Time.)
**Tone**: *Tactical, confident.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "Arm your team with battlecards for the big three objections. [pause 0.5s] When they say your software is too expensive, reframe the price against the cost of their manual workaround: 'Our six-thousand-dollar tool replaces forty thousand dollars in wasted payroll.'"
>
> "When they say they already use Excel, acknowledge Excel's strengths, then point out the lack of audit trails. [pause 0.5s] And when they say they don't have time to implement, prove that your onboarding takes under thirty minutes. Objections are not roadblocks; they are invitations to reframe value."
>

### [SLIDE 4 AUDIO] — Sales Playbooks in COSA Sales CRM (25s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 4 (Slide 4: COSA CRM deal card with sidebar playbook assistant showing objection scripts.)
**Tone**: *Technical, practical.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "In COSA Sales CRM, your playbook is not a dusty document buried in Google Drive. [pause 0.5s] It is embedded directly inside every active deal card. The system prompts reps with stage exit criteria, suggests relevant battlecards, and generates customized proposals in sixty seconds."
>
> "When sales process and software tooling live together, new reps ramp to full quota in weeks instead of months."
>

### [SLIDE 5 AUDIO] — Anti-Patterns vs. Best Practices (25s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 5 (Slide 5: Contrast table showing rogue sales discounting versus disciplined playbook adherence.)
**Tone**: *Cautionary, mentoring.*

> **Spoken Script**:

>
> "That creates operational chaos. [pause 0.5s] Do not hire your first sales rep until your sales playbook has been tested, refined, and proven by you personally. Require strict adherence to the process."
>

### [SLIDE 6 AUDIO] — Founder Action Checkpoint (25s)
