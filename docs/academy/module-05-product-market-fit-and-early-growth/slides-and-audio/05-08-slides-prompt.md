### Slide 3: The Decision Rights Matrix (DACI) (Governance Framework)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-02 — Definition Contrast`
- **Layout & Composition**: Two-panel split: Consensus Fog vs. Clear Decision Rights.
- **Header Badge**: `DECISION GOVERNANCE`
- **Main Headline**: **Consensus Paralysis vs. Explicit Decision Rights**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Why making decisions by committee destroys early startup momentum.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - The Consensus Trap (Slow): Everyone must agree on every detail. (Result: Endless debates, watered-down compromises, paralyzed progress).
  - The DACI Framework (Fast & Accountable):
  -   - Driver (D): One person who moves the initiative forward.
  -   - Approver (A): Exactly ONE person who has the final veto/approval power.
  -   - Contributors (C): People consulted for expert advice.
  -   - Informed (I): People notified of the final decision.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: GOVERNANCE LAW: Exactly ONE Approver per decision. If two people share approval power, nobody is truly accountable.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Split visual: Left shows tangled multi-headed committee arrows; right shows crisp single illuminated spearhead.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Two column contrast visual on dark navy: Left shows chaotic swirling red arrows; right shows single focused glowing teal spearhead piercing a target.*
