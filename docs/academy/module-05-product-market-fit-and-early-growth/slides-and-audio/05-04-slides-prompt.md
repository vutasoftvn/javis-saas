### Slide 2: The 4 Channel Scorecard Dimensions (Evaluation Framework)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-04 — Focus Framework`
- **Layout & Composition**: 4-part container layout on surface #0D172A.
- **Header Badge**: `CHANNEL SCORECARD`
- **Main Headline**: **The 4 Dimensions of Channel Quality**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Benchmark every acquisition channel across these four operational criteria.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - 1. Fully-Loaded CAC: Direct ad spend + agency fees + founder sales hours required to close one customer.
  - 2. Pipeline Velocity: Average number of days from first touch to signed contract. (Faster channels preserve cash).
  - 3. Customer Quality / LTV: Average Annual Contract Value (ACV) and expansion potential generated by leads from this channel.
  - 4. Month-6 Retention Rate: Percentage of customers acquired from this specific channel who remain active after 180 days.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: THE CHANNEL TRAP: Paid social ads often look cheap on CAC, but suffer 3x higher churn than outbound email or organic content.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Four horizontal cards showing CAC, Velocity Days, LTV Multiplier, and Retention % with comparative rating badges.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Four sleek glass cards in horizontal alignment on deep navy canvas, glowing cyan metrics and comparative rating stars.*

### Slide 3: Channel Reallocation in Practice (Strategic Reallocation)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-02 — Definition Contrast`
- **Layout & Composition**: Two-panel split: Channel A (Cheap/Leaky) vs. Channel B (Targeted/Sticky).
- **Header Badge**: `CAPITAL ALLOCATION`
- **Main Headline**: **The Low-Cost Trap vs. The High-Value Channel**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: A real-world case study in marketing capital reallocation.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Channel A (Paid Facebook / Google Ads): $150 CAC, 50 signups/mo. Sounds great! (Reality: 8% Month-6 retention; negative LTV:CAC).
  - Channel B (Targeted LinkedIn Outbound): $800 CAC, 10 signups/mo. Feels expensive! (Reality: 85% Month-6 retention; $12,000 LTV).
  - The Strategic Decision: Kill Channel A immediately. Reallocate 100% of budget and time to scaling Channel B.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: ALLOCATION RULE: Always pay more to acquire higher-quality customers who stick around for years.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Split visual: Left shows leaky plastic pipe pouring into empty red drain; right shows solid copper pipeline filling gold safe.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Two column contrast visual on dark navy: Left shows leaking red pipe with small numbers; right shows solid glowing gold conduit delivering heavy bullion.*

### Slide 5: Anti-Patterns vs. Best Practices (Comparative Matrix)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-06 — Decision Checkpoint`
- **Layout & Composition**: Side-by-side comparison table.
- **Header Badge**: `ACQUISITION PITFALLS`
