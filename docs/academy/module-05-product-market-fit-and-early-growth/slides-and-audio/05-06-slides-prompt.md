### Slide 5: Anti-Patterns vs. Best Practices (Comparative Matrix)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-06 — Decision Checkpoint`
- **Layout & Composition**: Side-by-side comparison table.
- **Header Badge**: `EXPERIMENTATION TRAPS`
- **Main Headline**: **Testing Too Many Variables vs. Isolated Micro-Tests**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Avoiding the experimental confusion that invalidates growth data.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Trap: Changing the landing page headline, pricing, button color, and target audience all in the same week. (Impossible to isolate cause).
  - Trap: Running experiments with sample sizes so tiny that results lack statistical significance.
  - Best Practice: Change exactly ONE isolated variable per experiment; run tests until minimum sample size is reached.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: DECISION CHECKPOINT: Isolate your variable. If you change three things at once, you will never know which one drove the result.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Table comparing multi-variable chaos with isolated, single-variable experimental discipline.
