- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-04 — Focus Framework`
- **Layout & Composition**: 3-column container comparison on surface #0D172A.
- **Header Badge**: `PMF METRICS`
- **Main Headline**: **The 3 Mathematical Proofs of Product-Market Fit**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: The three objective metrics that define genuine PMF.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - 1. The Sean Ellis 40% Test: 'How would you feel if you could no longer use COSA?' → >40% of surveyed active users must answer: 'Very Disappointed'.
  - 2. The Asymptotic Retention Curve: Cohort retention curves flatten and remain parallel to the x-axis after 90 days (zero ongoing decay).
  - 3. Organic Word-of-Mouth Coefficient: >30% of new customer signups originate from unprompted customer referrals or peer recommendations.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: BENCHMARK: If 'Very Disappointed' is under 40%, you do NOT have PMF. Stop marketing and return to customer feedback.

### Slide 3: False PMF vs. Genuine Market Pull (Diagnostic Contrast)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-02 — Definition Contrast`
- **Layout & Composition**: Two-panel comparative card layout: Fake PMF vs. Real PMF.
- **Header Badge**: `DIAGNOSTIC CONTRAST`
- **Main Headline**: **Vanity Illusions vs. True Market Pull**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Distinguishing artificial marketing spikes from organic product adoption.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - False PMF (The Vanity Mirage): Massive spike in signups driven by PR or heavy paid ad spend, followed by an 80% drop-off in month two.
  - Genuine PMF (The Compounding Engine): Moderate, steady user acquisition, but users log in every single day and invite their colleagues.
  - The Danger: Spending growth capital on False PMF simply accelerates the burn of cash reserves into a leaky bucket.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: WARNING: High signup volume with low retention is not growth; it is an expensive churn machine.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Split visual: Left side shows a sharp volcanic red spike that crashes to zero; right side shows a steady, glowing teal exponential curve.
