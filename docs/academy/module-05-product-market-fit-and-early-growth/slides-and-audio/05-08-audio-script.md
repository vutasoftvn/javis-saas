**Slide Reference**: Slide 3 (Slide 3: Chaotic swirling red arrows vs. single focused glowing teal spearhead.)

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "Never make decisions by consensus. [pause 0.5s] When everyone has to agree on everything, meetings drag on for hours, bold ideas get watered down, and execution grinds to a halt."
>
> "Use the **DACI Framework**. [pause 0.5s] Every initiative has one Driver moving it forward, and exactly ONE Approver who makes the final call. Consult experts for input, but make sure one person has the ultimate authority and accountability to decide."
>
