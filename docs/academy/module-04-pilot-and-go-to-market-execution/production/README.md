# Module 4 — Seedance Production Pack

## Production decision

Module 4 has **nine lessons**, each assembled from **three complete 28-second Seedance videos**. Every render is a single English-speaking founder-led scene with synchronized voice, music, sound design, and an in-frame conceptual slide when it helps. Never split founder and slide into separate assets; never generate real COSA UI, dashboards, CRM screens, or walkthroughs.

1. **Hook:** founder focus, no slide, founder fills 65–70% of frame.
2. **Pilot model:** founder left 38%, teaching screen right 62%.
3. **Practice or decision:** founder left 38%, action screen right 62%.

Each lesson is approximately **84 seconds** after joining the three clips with a 0.4–0.6-second soft dissolve or teal-light sweep.

## Series continuity

Keep the approved host from Modules 0–3: Vietnamese male founder and operating coach, around 40, short dark hair, dark smart-casual overshirt, neutral shirt, polished editorial illustration. Keep the same face, wardrobe, English voice, camera language, and 16:9 frame; reuse a prior approved reference still in Seedance when possible.

The setting remains COSA deep navy, now with a subtle **pilot operations studio**: distant customer journey lines, a small teal review loop, soft operational check marks, and warm desk light. These are decorative illustrations only—not task UI, data dashboards, CRM, or an app screen.

## Audio and edit language

- Native-level English male voice around 40, warm measured delivery, neutral international accent, exact script and natural lip sync.
- Minimal warm electronic ambient music at 78 BPM, no vocals or heavy beat, lower than voice.
- Gentle light-whoosh for slide appearance, quiet evidence tones and restrained action resolve tone only.
- No notifications, typing, crowd, applause, dramatic impacts, unscripted dialogue or unrelated effects.
- Each clip has 0.75-second handles at start and end; keep bottom 18% clear for English captions added in post.

## Teaching screens

Use the [COSA-derived in-frame slide system](../../module-00-founder-foundations/production/slide-system.md): deep navy, white hierarchy, teal action, sky-blue learning/evidence and rose warnings. One takeaway plus one simple model only; never UI.
