### Slide 2: The 4-Question Friday Review Protocol (Operating Process)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-04 — Focus Framework`
- **Layout & Composition**: 4-part horizontal process card stack on surface #0D172A.
- **Header Badge**: `WEEKLY PROTOCOL`
- **Main Headline**: **The 4 Questions of the Friday Pilot Review**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: A disciplined retrospective protocol executed every Friday at 4 PM.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - 1. What Actually Happened? (Telemetry Audit): Review logins, task completions, and inactive accounts across all active pilots.
  - 2. Why Did It Happen? (Root-Cause Inquiry): Investigate anomalies. Why did Account B drop by 40%? Why did Account A surge?
  - 3. What Changed for the Customer? (Value Verification): Calculate cumulative hours saved and operational errors prevented this week.
  - 4. What Action Is Next? (Sprint Prioritization): Generate 3 engineering fixes or support tasks for Monday morning.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: OPERATIONAL DISCIPLINE: Spend 10 minutes on data review, 15 minutes on customer interviews, 20 minutes on sprint action planning.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Four horizontal cards showing Telemetry Audit, Root-Cause, Value Delta, and Next Actions with glowing connectors.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Four sleek glass cards on deep navy canvas, progressive numbering, icons for Bar Chart, Magnifying Glass, Shield, and Next Arrow.*

### Slide 3: Anomaly Detection: Catching Drift Early (Diagnostic Methodology)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-02 — Definition Contrast`
- **Layout & Composition**: Split comparative card layout: Normal Trajectory vs. Silent Drift.
- **Header Badge**: `DRIFT DIAGNOSTICS`
- **Main Headline**: **Normal Adoption vs. The Silent Drift Trap**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Identifying the subtle behavioral signals that precede account abandonment.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Healthy Adoption (Compounding): Session frequency increases week-over-week; new team members get added organically.
  - Silent Drift (Danger Signal): Total logins stay flat, but the primary champion stops logging in, delegating usage to an intern.
  - The Early Action: Drift indicates that the core mechanism failed to deliver executive-level value. Schedule an emergency triage call.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: WARNING: When your primary executive champion stops logging in, your pilot is dying, even if their team is still active.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Split visual: Left shows healthy upward green line; right shows declining amber trajectory with blinking red warning blip.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Two column contrast visual on dark navy: Left shows steady ascending green line; right shows descending amber curve triggering red alert.*
