- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-04 — Focus Framework`

  - 4. STOP (Kill the Bet): Users completed the workflows but expressed zero willingness to pay or retain. Archive the initiative and preserve capital.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: STOP COURAGE: Ending a failed pilot protects your runway from being drained by zombie accounts that will never convert.
