- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-04 — Focus Framework`
- **Layout & Composition**: 5-card container layout on surface #0D172A.
- **Header Badge**: `PILOT DESIGN BLUEPRINT`
- **Main Headline**: **The 5 Structural Pillars of a Controlled Pilot**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Every pilot engagement must define these five parameters before kickoff.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - 1. Target Customer Cohort: Exactly 3 to 5 committed accounts sharing identical beachhead characteristics.
  - 2. Strict Scope Perimeter: The single workflow or department using the tool (excluding adjacent company systems).
  - 3. Fixed Timeframe: A 30-day to 45-day window with an explicit 'Evaluation Day' on the calendar.
  - 4. Joint Success Metrics: 2 quantifiable operational outcomes agreed upon by both the founder and customer sponsor.
  - 5. Commercial Conversion Trigger: Pre-signed contract clause agreeing to annual purchase upon hitting success metrics.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: COHORT DISCIPLINE: Run 3 to 5 pilots simultaneously in a single cohort to compare results across accounts.
