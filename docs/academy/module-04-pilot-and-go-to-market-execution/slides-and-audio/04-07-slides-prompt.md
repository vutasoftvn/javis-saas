- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-04 — Focus Framework`
- **Layout & Composition**: 5-card interlocking horizontal pipeline on surface #0D172A.
- **Header Badge**: `GTM ENGINE ARCHITECTURE`
- **Main Headline**: **The 5 Structural Gears of a Scalable GTM Motion**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Aligning every customer touchpoint into one continuous operational pipeline.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Gear 1: The Beachhead Segment — Narrow operational role with urgent, budget-backed pain.
  - Gear 2: The Core Market Message — Jargon-free value proposition supported by verified pilot case studies.
  - Gear 3: The Primary Distribution Channel — Focused outbound, inbound, or ecosystem partnership motion.
  - Gear 4: The Sales Playbook — 5-stage qualification process in Sales CRM with standard proposal templates.
  - Gear 5: The Onboarding Cadence — Automated workflow ensuring Time-to-First-Value <30 minutes.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: ENGINE INTEGRATION: If Gear 4 (Sales) promises features that Gear 5 (Onboarding) cannot deliver, the engine jams and churn explodes.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Five interlocking gears arranged horizontally with glowing cyan and gold teeth.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Five interconnected mechanical gears on deep navy canvas, progressive size, glowing teal and gold highlights, clean typography.*

### Slide 3: Sales Motion Architecture: Self-Serve vs. Inside Sales (Motion Taxonomy)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-02 — Definition Contrast`
- **Layout & Composition**: Two-panel split: Self-Serve Product-Led vs. High-Touch Inside Sales.
- **Header Badge**: `SALES MOTION`
- **Main Headline**: **Self-Serve Frictionless vs. High-Touch Consultation**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Matching your sales motion to your customer's buying complexity.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Self-Serve Motion (Low ACV <$2k/yr): Frictionless credit card checkout, interactive in-app onboarding tours, zero human intervention.
  - Inside Sales Motion (Mid-High ACV >$10k/yr): 30-minute discovery demo, customized ROI business case, security compliance review, formal contract.
  - The Hybrid Mistake: Requiring a demo call for a $20/month tool, or expecting enterprise buyers to swipe a card on a landing page.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: MOTION RULE: Respect customer buying habits. Match human touch directly to deal size.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Split visual: Left side shows instant credit card checkout click; right side shows professional consultation handshake.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Two column contrast visual on dark navy: Left shows digital 1-click checkout button; right shows two executives reviewing a glowing digital contract.*

- **Header Badge**: `COSA IMPLEMENTATION`

  - Workflows Automation: Automatically trigger client onboarding sequences the instant a deal closes.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: SYSTEM HARMONY: When marketing, sales, and onboarding live in one operating system, customer handover is seamless.

### Slide 5: Anti-Patterns vs. Best Practices (Comparative Matrix)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-06 — Decision Checkpoint`
- **Layout & Composition**: Side-by-side comparison table.
- **Header Badge**: `GTM PITFALLS`
- **Main Headline**: **The 'Build It and They Will Come' Trap vs. Disciplined GTM**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Why brilliant software never sells itself without a structured distribution machine.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Trap: Assuming a great product will create viral word-of-mouth with zero proactive sales effort.
  - Trap: Hiring expensive junior sales reps before the founder masters the sales motion personally.
  - Best Practice: Founder-led sales first! The founder must close the first 10 to 20 customers before handing off the playbook.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: FOUNDER RULE: If the founder cannot sell the product, no hired salesperson ever will.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Table comparing passive product-led waiting with active founder-led GTM execution.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Comparison table on dark canvas: red hazard badges next to passive product waiting; teal checkmarks next to disciplined founder-led sales execution.*
