### [SLIDE 1 AUDIO] — Title & Core Thesis (25s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 1 (Slide 1: Circular radar screen evaluating Acquisition, Retention, Delivery, and Margin.)
**Tone**: *Rigorous, diagnostic, strategic.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "You ran a successful pilot and converted three customers. That is a fantastic milestone, but be very careful: a successful pilot does NOT automatically mean you have Product-Market Fit."
>
> "A pilot proves that you can create value when the founder is standing right there holding the customer's hand. [pause 0.5s] In Lesson 4.8, you will audit your **PMF Readiness**. You will verify whether customer demand and retention can repeat autonomously without founder subsidies."
>

### [SLIDE 2 AUDIO] — The 4 Pillars of PMF Readiness (30s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 2 (Slide 2: Four vertical cards showing Acquisition, Retention, Delivery, and Margins.)
**Tone**: *Instructional, structured.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "To enter the growth phase, you must satisfy four readiness pillars. [pause 0.5s] First, **Repeatable Acquisition**: can you find and close a stranger through your sales channel without personal favors?"
>
> "Second, **Spontaneous Retention**: do they keep using the software when you don't call them? [pause 0.5s] Third, **Standardized Delivery**: can a team member onboard a customer without the founder? And fourth, **Unit Margins**: do your gross margins stay above seventy percent as volume grows? If any pillar fails, scaling will crush you."
>

### [SLIDE 3 AUDIO] — The Pilot Illusion: Founder Subsidies (25s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 3 (Slide 3: Founder straining under heavy stone arch vs. autonomous steel structure.)
**Tone**: *Sharp, eye-opening.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "Beware of the **Founder Subsidy Trap**. [pause 0.5s] Often, a pilot is successful only because the founder spent twenty hours a week doing custom data clean-up, hand-holding, and personal consulting for the client."
>
> "Ask yourself the ultimate question: [pause 0.5s] if you disappeared on vacation for two weeks with no laptop, would the customer keep using the software and pay their invoice? If the answer is no, you don't have a software product; you have an outsourced consulting agency."
>

### [SLIDE 4 AUDIO] — PMF Readiness Scorecard in COSA Strategy (25s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 4 (Slide 4: COSA PMF Scorecard modal showing 78% Readiness score and Growth Ready tag.)
**Tone**: *Technical, practical.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "In COSA Strategy, your PMF Readiness Scorecard audits your company's operational maturity. [pause 0.5s] It synthesizes your CRM conversion data, customer support tickets, and financial gross margins."
>
> "COSA calculates your official Readiness Index. [pause 0.5s] It protects you from the fatal temptation of pouring money into Facebook ads or hiring a sales force before your underlying product engine is truly stable."
>

### [SLIDE 5 AUDIO] — Anti-Patterns vs. Best Practices (25s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 5 (Slide 5: Contrast table showing premature growth acceleration versus disciplined readiness.)
**Tone**: *Cautionary, mentoring.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "Do not rush to scale prematurely. [pause 0.5s] When founders raise capital and start pouring money into customer acquisition before their product retains autonomously, they are setting fire to their runway."
>
> "Take the time to eliminate founder dependencies. [pause 0.5s] Document your onboarding playbooks, automate data syncs, and ensure the software itself delivers the value. When your product retains customers on its own merits, you are ready to conquer the market."
>

### [SLIDE 6 AUDIO] — Founder Action Checkpoint (25s)

>
