**Slide Reference**: Slide 2 (Slide 2: Five interlocking gears showing Beachhead, Message, Channel, Sales, and Onboarding.)
**Tone**: *Instructional, architectural.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "A scalable GTM engine has five interlocking gears. [pause 0.5s] First, the narrow beachhead segment. Second, your proven core market message—backed by your pilot case studies."
>
> "Third, your single primary distribution channel. [pause 0.5s] Fourth, your standardized sales playbook in Sales CRM. And fifth, your onboarding cadence to ensure lightning-fast time to value. If any one of these gears slips, your commercial engine grinds to a halt."
>

### [SLIDE 3 AUDIO] — Sales Motion Architecture: Self-Serve vs. Inside Sales (25s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 3 (Slide 3: Instant 1-click checkout button vs. executives reviewing digital contract.)
**Tone**: *Analytical, precise.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "Match your sales motion to your customer's buying habits. [pause 0.5s] If you sell an affordable self-serve productivity tool, keep your checkout completely frictionless. Don't force them onto a thirty-minute demo call."
>
> "Conversely, if you sell a twenty-thousand-dollar software platform to enterprise departments, accept that they need executive consultation, security reviews, and custom agreements. [pause 0.5s] Match human touch directly to contract value."
>

### [SLIDE 4 AUDIO] — GTM Orchestration in COSA Marketing & Sales (25s)

### [SLIDE 5 AUDIO] — Anti-Patterns vs. Best Practices (25s)
