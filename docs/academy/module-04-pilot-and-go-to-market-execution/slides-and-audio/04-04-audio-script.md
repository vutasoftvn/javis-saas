**Slide Reference**: Slide 2 (Slide 2: Four cards showing Telemetry Audit, Root-Cause, Value Delta, and Next Actions.)
**Tone**: *Instructional, structured.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "Run your Friday review on four precise questions. [pause 0.5s] First: what actually happened? Audit your telemetry—how many workflows were completed across each account?"
