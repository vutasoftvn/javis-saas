### Slide 2: The 4 Classes of Customer Alternatives (Taxonomy Matrix)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-04 — Focus Framework`
- **Layout & Composition**: 4-card structured grid on surface #0D172A.
- **Header Badge**: `ALTERNATIVE TAXONOMY`
- **Main Headline**: **The 4 Classes of Competition**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Mapping the complete spectrum of options available to your prospective buyer.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - 1. Direct Competitors: Software products targeting the exact same job with similar technology (e.g., Salesforce vs. HubSpot).
  - 2. Indirect Competitors: Different product categories that solve the same underlying job (e.g., Zoom competing with airline tickets).
  - 3. Manual Workarounds: Google Sheets, Notion databases, paper checklists, and custom Python scripts.
  - 4. Non-Consumption (The Default): Choosing to tolerate the pain, delay the decision, or do nothing at all.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: DISCOVERY REALITY: In 80% of B2B deals, you lose to Category 3 (Spreadsheets) and Category 4 (Doing Nothing).
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Four distinct cards in 2x2 grid with icon badges for Direct Software, Indirect Alternatives, Spreadsheets, and Couch/Inertia.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *2x2 modern card grid on dark slate background, glowing teal borders, minimalist icons representing app battle, airplane, spreadsheet, and sleeping clock.*

### Slide 3: The Trade-Off Matrix (Analytical Comparison)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-02 — Definition Contrast`
- **Layout & Composition**: Comparative attribute table comparing Status Quo vs. New Solution.
- **Header Badge**: `VALUE EQUATION`
- **Main Headline**: **The Customer's Trade-Off Equation**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Why superior features fail if switching costs and learning curves are too high.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - The Existing Workaround (Spreadsheets): Free, fully customizable, everyone knows how to use it, zero procurement delay. (Cons: Error-prone, slow).
  - Your Proposed Solution: Fast, automated, error-free. (Cons: Costs money, requires new habits, migration friction).
  - The 10x Value Rule: To overcome customer inertia, your solution must be 10x better on at least ONE dimension that the customer values urgently.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: POSITIONING LAW: You do not have to be better at everything; you must be 10x better at the one job that matters most.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Balance scale comparison showing the massive gravitational weight of Spreadsheets balanced against a concentrated 10x glowing teal advantage.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Stylized balance visual on dark canvas: heavy grey spreadsheet ledger balanced against a concentrated, glowing cyan power core.*

### Slide 5: Anti-Patterns vs. Best Practices (Comparative Matrix)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-06 — Decision Checkpoint`
- **Layout & Composition**: Side-by-side comparison table.
- **Header Badge**: `COMPETITIVE BLINDSPOTS`
- **Main Headline**: **The 'We Have No Competitors' Fallacy**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Why claiming zero competition instantly destroys investor and customer credibility.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Trap: Telling investors 'Nobody does what we do.' (Signals that you haven't researched how customers currently solve the job).
  - Trap: Obsessing over direct startup competitors and copying their feature releases feature-for-feature.

- **Highlight / Accent Box**: DECISION CHECKPOINT: If you cannot name the exact spreadsheet or habit your product will replace, you don't understand the market.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Table comparing naive competitive denial with mature status-quo analysis.
