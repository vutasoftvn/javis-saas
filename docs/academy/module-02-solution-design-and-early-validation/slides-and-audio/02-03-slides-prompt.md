## SLIDE-BY-SLIDE SPECIFICATIONS

### Slide 1: Title & Core Thesis (Hero Presentation)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-01 — Takeaway Claim`
- **Layout & Composition**: Hero layout with test-tube experiment gauge on #070C18 canvas.
- **Header Badge**: `COSA ACADEMY · MODULE 02 · LESSON 2.3`
- **Main Headline**: **Running Solution Tests: Evidence Over Hope**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Designing rigorous experiment protocols with upfront success criteria before placing your solution in front of users.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Testing without predefined decision rules leads to rationalizing failure as 'almost working'.
  - Different solution assumptions require different test methods: usability test, concierge pilot, or smoke test.
  - Every experiment must yield an unambiguous binary answer: Does the solution create value, or does it not?
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: EXPERIMENT LAW: Define what success looks like in advance; otherwise, you will declare victory regardless of what happens.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Visual laboratory gauge: An illuminated measurement meter on dark background with clear green pass and red fail thresholds.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Sleek modern digital gauge meter on dark slate #070C18, glowing teal needle pointing at clear numerical pass threshold.*

### Slide 2: The 4 Solution Testing Methods (Taxonomy & Protocol)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-04 — Focus Framework`
- **Layout & Composition**: 4-column container matrix on surface #0D172A.
- **Header Badge**: `TESTING TOOLKIT`
- **Main Headline**: **The 4 Standard Solution Test Methods**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Match your specific risk to the appropriate experimental vehicle.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - 1. Usability Task Test: Watch 5 users attempt to complete the core job without any coaching. Measure task completion rate.
  - 2. Concierge Value Test: Deliver the outcome manually for 3 clients. Measure whether they express gratitude and demand to keep using it.
  - 3. Smoke / Fake-Door Test: Measure click-through intent on a specific feature button before coding the back-end.
  - 4. Pre-Commitment Test: Ask the user to sign a non-binding letter of intent or schedule their data migration call.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: METHOD RULE: Choose Usability for UX friction, Concierge for value proof, Pre-Commitment for commercial interest.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Four vertical cards with glowing icons for Stopwatch, Hands, Door, and Signature.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Four sleek glass cards on dark background, glowing teal icons, clear headers for Usability, Concierge, Smoke, and Pre-Commitment.*

### Slide 3: Pre-Defining the Decision Rule (Decision Governance)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-06 — Decision Checkpoint`
- **Layout & Composition**: Two-panel split: Binary Threshold Specification.
- **Header Badge**: `DECISION RULES`
- **Main Headline**: **The Binary Decision Rule Blueprint**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Locking in your quantitative criteria to eliminate founder rationalization.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Sample Size: Exactly 10 target beachhead prospects.
  - The Primary Metric: Unassisted task completion resulting in the core job outcome.
  - Pass Criterion (Green): At least 7 out of 10 successfully complete the job in under 15 minutes without human assistance.
  - Fail Criterion (Red): Fewer than 5 out of 10 succeed, or users require constant founder intervention to navigate the flow.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: SCIENTIFIC RIGOR: If 4 out of 10 pass, the test is a FAIL. Redesign the mechanism before expanding testing.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Split card: Left shows green pass threshold (>7/10); right shows red fail threshold (<5/10).
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Clean comparison cards on dark canvas: glowing green border for pass criteria; glowing crimson border for failure trigger.*

### Slide 4: Experiment Setup in COSA Projects (COSA Workspace Integration)

- **Layout & Composition**: UI card preview of COSA Experiment Tracker.
- **Header Badge**: `COSA IMPLEMENTATION`

- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Recording test plans, cohorts, and results in your Project knowledge base.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Experiment Brief: Document the hypothesis, method, audience, and decision rule.
  - Cohort Tracker: Log each participant's session notes, completion time, and hesitation points.
  - Automated Scorecard: COSA tallies pass/fail ratios and prompts the founder for the next stage decision.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: DATA GOVERNANCE: Link screen recordings and raw test logs directly into COSA Vault for auditability.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Mockup of COSA Experiment Brief card with live progress bar and candidate participant ledger.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Modern UI card mockup on dark slate #070C18, showing experiment title, participant count (8/10), and pass status badge.*

### Slide 5: Anti-Patterns vs. Best Practices (Comparative Matrix)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-06 — Decision Checkpoint`
- **Layout & Composition**: Side-by-side comparison table.
- **Header Badge**: `EXPERIMENT PITFALLS`
- **Main Headline**: **Interfering in Tests vs. Silent Observation**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: How founders accidentally invalidate their own experiment data.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Trap: Helping the user when they get stuck ('Oh, you just need to click that little blue icon there!').
  - Trap: Counting compliments ('They said it looks amazing!') instead of actual task success.
  - Best Practice: Sit on your hands, remain completely silent, and watch where the user stumbles.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: TESTING RULE: If you have to explain how to use your MVP during the test, the test has failed.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Table contrasting founder interference with disciplined scientific observation.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Comparison table on dark canvas: red hazard badges next to founder back-seat driving; teal checkmarks next to silent observation.*
