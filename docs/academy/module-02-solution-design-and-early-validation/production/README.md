# Module 2 — Seedance Production Pack

## Production decision

Module 2 contains **nine lessons**, each made from **three complete 28-second Seedance videos**. Every render is an English-speaking founder-led scene with synchronized voice, music, sound design, and—when useful—one in-frame teaching slide. Do not produce detached founder clips, detached slides, fake COSA screens, or product walkthroughs.

1. **Hook:** founder focus, no slide, founder fills 65–70% of frame.
2. **Test model:** founder left 38%, conceptual teaching screen right 62%.
3. **Practice:** founder left 38%, example or decision screen right 62%, ending in a learner action.

Each lesson is approximately **84 seconds** after joining its three clips with a 0.4–0.6-second soft dissolve or teal-light sweep.

## Series continuity

Reuse the approved recurring host from Modules 0–1: Vietnamese male founder and operating coach, around 40, short dark hair, dark smart-casual overshirt, neutral shirt, polished editorial illustration, warm and credible. Keep the same face, wardrobe, voice, 16:9 framing, and camera language; use an approved prior still as reference in Seedance whenever possible.

The setting is the same COSA deep-navy studio, now with subtle **solution testing studio** atmosphere: distant abstract prototype sheets, a small teal experiment path, soft quote marks and warm desk light. This background is decorative only—not a real product, dashboard, wireframe tool, or interface.

## Audio and edit language

- Native-level English male voice, around 40, warm measured delivery, neutral international accent, exact script and natural lip sync.
- Minimal warm electronic ambient music at 78 BPM, no vocals and no strong beat; voice remains clearly dominant.
- Sound effects are limited to a soft light-whoosh for slides, subtle evidence tones, and a restrained resolve tone for action.
- No typing, notifications, crowd, applause, dramatic impacts, invented dialogue, or unrelated sound effects.
- Keep a 0.75-second visual handle at the beginning and end of every clip. Reserve the lower 18% for English captions added in post.

## Teaching screens

Teaching screens follow the COSA-derived visual system in Module 0’s [in-frame slide system](../../module-00-founder-foundations/production/slide-system.md): deep navy canvas, white hierarchy, teal action, sky-blue learning/evidence, rose warning. Use one takeaway and one simple model. Never imitate COSA’s product UI.
