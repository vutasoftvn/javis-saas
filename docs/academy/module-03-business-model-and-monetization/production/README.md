# Module 3 — Seedance Production Pack

## Production decision

Module 3 contains **nine lessons**, each made from **three complete 28-second Seedance videos**. Each render is a complete English-speaking founder-led scene: synchronized voice, music, sound design, and an in-frame teaching slide only when useful. Never create detached presenter/slide assets, real COSA screens, fake CRM or finance dashboards, or product walkthroughs.

1. **Hook:** founder focus, no slide, founder fills 65–70% of the frame.
2. **Commercial model:** founder left 38%, conceptual teaching screen right 62%.
3. **Practice or decision:** founder left 38%, action/decision screen right 62%.

Each lesson is about **84 seconds** after joining its three clips with a 0.4–0.6-second soft dissolve or teal-light sweep.

## Series continuity

Keep the approved recurring host from Modules 0–2: Vietnamese male founder and operating coach, around 40, short dark hair, dark smart-casual overshirt, neutral shirt, polished editorial illustration, warm and credible. Reuse an approved founder reference still whenever Seedance allows. Never change face, wardrobe, voice, framing, or visual style.

Use the COSA deep-navy studio with subtle **commercial studio** texture: distant abstract buyer-path lines, restrained ledger marks, a small teal experiment trail, and warm desk light. This is ambient illustration only—not a financial dashboard, CRM screen, spreadsheet, interface, or data visualization.

## Audio and edit language

- Native-level English male voice, about 40, warm measured delivery, neutral international accent, exact script and natural lip sync.
- Minimal warm electronic ambient music at 78 BPM; no vocal and no heavy beat; voice clearly leads the mix.
- Soft light-whoosh for teaching screens, quiet evidence tones, and a restrained resolve tone for learner action only.
- No typing, notifications, crowd, applause, dramatic impacts, unscripted dialogue, or unrelated effects.
- Preserve a 0.75-second visual handle at each end. Keep lower 18% clear for English captions added in post.

## Teaching screens

Use Module 0’s [COSA-derived in-frame slide system](../../module-00-founder-foundations/production/slide-system.md): deep navy canvas, white hierarchy, teal action, sky-blue learning/evidence and rose warning. Screens communicate one takeaway and one simple business model, never product UI.
