### Slide 2: The 5 Major Software Business Models (Taxonomy Matrix)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-04 — Focus Framework`
- **Layout & Composition**: 5-card container layout on surface #0D172A.
- **Header Badge**: `BUSINESS MODEL TAXONOMY`
- **Main Headline**: **The 5 Primary Monetization Patterns**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Deconstructing the mechanics, advantages, and risks of standard revenue architectures.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - 1. SaaS Subscription (Per User / Per Month): Predictable recurring revenue; requires high ongoing retention.
  - 2. Usage-Based / Metered (Pay-As-You-Go): Aligns price directly with volume; low barrier to entry, but revenue fluctuates.
  - 3. Transaction / Take-Rate: Takes 1-3% of gross merchandise volume (GMV); ideal for fintech, marketplaces, and payment flows.
  - 4. Marketplace Commission: Charges matching or processing fees to buyers or sellers; requires dual-sided liquidity.
  - 5. Service-Assisted Hybrid: High-touch manual onboarding or custom setup fees paired with software subscriptions.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: PATTERN RULE: Start with simple flat pricing or tiered subscription before inventing complex usage-based formulas.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Five vertical cards with distinct icon badges for Calendar (SaaS), Meter (Usage), Percentage (Take-rate), Scales (Marketplace), and Tools (Hybrid).
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Five sleek glassmorphic cards on dark background, glowing gold and teal icon badges, clean typography.*

### Slide 3: Aligning Model to Customer Behavior (Strategic Alignment)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-02 — Definition Contrast`
- **Layout & Composition**: Two-panel comparative card layout: Natural Fit vs. Friction Fit.
- **Header Badge**: `ALIGNMENT CHECK`
- **Main Headline**: **Value Cadence vs. Billing Cadence**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Why forcing an incompatible billing model creates massive customer resistance.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Natural Alignment: Monthly recurring billing for daily operational software (e.g., payroll, CRM, communication).
  - Misaligned Friction: Charging a $50/month subscription for a tool used once every six months (e.g., annual tax filing, house hunting).
  - The Rule of Frequency: Infrequent high-value events require transactional or success fees; daily/weekly habits support subscriptions.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: ALIGNMENT TEST: If customers don't log in at least weekly, an annual or monthly subscription will suffer brutal churn.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Split visual: Left side shows smooth flowing gold pipeline; right side shows jammed pipeline leaking red cash drops.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Two column contrast visual on dark navy: Left shows frictionless glowing gold conduit; right shows blocked pipeline with red hazard leak.*

### Slide 5: Anti-Patterns vs. Best Practices (Comparative Matrix)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-06 — Decision Checkpoint`
- **Layout & Composition**: Side-by-side comparison table.
- **Header Badge**: `MONETIZATION PITFALLS`
- **Main Headline**: **The 'Freemium Fantasy' vs. Value Capture**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Avoiding the common monetization traps that bankrupt early ventures.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Trap: Offering an overly generous free tier hoping users will magically upgrade later. (Free users consume server costs and never convert).
  - Trap: Underpricing out of fear ('We'll charge $5/month so nobody says no'). (Signals low quality and breaks unit economics).
  - Best Practice: Charge early, charge direct, and validate willingness to pay before writing automated billing code.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: DECISION CHECKPOINT: If nobody complains about your price, you are charging way too little.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Table comparing freemium dilution traps with disciplined direct value capture.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Comparison table on dark canvas: red hazard badges next to free-tier traps; teal checkmarks next to disciplined direct pricing.*
