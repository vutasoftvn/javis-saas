### Slide 2: The Van Westendorp Price Sensitivity Model (Pricing Methodology)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-04 — Focus Framework`
- **Layout & Composition**: 4-part analytical question container stack on surface #0D172A.
- **Header Badge**: `PRICE SENSITIVITY FRAMEWORK`
- **Main Headline**: **The 4 Van Westendorp Pricing Questions**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: A scientific method to map the acceptable price range for your product.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Question 1 (Too Cheap): 'At what price would this be so cheap that you would question its reliability and security?'
  - Question 2 (Bargain): 'At what price would you consider this a great buy and swipe your card immediately?'
  - Question 3 (Expensive): 'At what price does this feel expensive, but you would still consider purchasing it?'
  - Question 4 (Too Expensive): 'At what price is this so expensive that you would walk away without a second thought?'
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: OPTIMAL PRICE POINT: The sweet spot lies between 'Expensive' (high margin) and 'Too Expensive' (deal breaker).
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Four horizontal cards showing the 4 price thresholds with color-coded benchmark indicators.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Four stacked horizontal data cards on deep navy, labeled Too Cheap, Bargain, Expensive, and Too Expensive with glowing cyan markers.*

### Slide 3: The 3 Practical Early-Stage Pricing Tests (Experiment Architecture)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-02 — Definition Contrast`
- **Layout & Composition**: 3-column container comparison: Proposals, Pre-Orders, Tiered Pitching.
- **Header Badge**: `EXPERIMENT PROTOCOLS`
- **Main Headline**: **The 3 Low-Risk Pricing Experiments**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: How to test pricing without burning prospective buyer relationships.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - 1. The Escalating Proposal Test: Quote $200/mo to Buyer 1, $400/mo to Buyer 2, and $800/mo to Buyer 3 until you encounter price resistance.
  - 2. The Paid Pilot Deposit: Offer a 30-day pilot for a non-refundable $1,000 setup deposit credited toward annual subscriptions.
  - 3. The 3-Tier Menu: Present Good ($299), Better ($599), and Best ($1,200). Observe which tier 80% of buyers select.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: ESCALATION RULE: Keep raising your price with each new sales conversation until 30% of qualified buyers push back.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Three vertical cards showing escalating price tags: $200, $400, and $800 with customer response gauges.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Three sleek glass cards on dark canvas, displaying glowing price tags $200, $400, $800 with green, teal, and amber status pills.*

### Slide 4: Pricing Experiments in COSA Sales CRM (COSA Workspace Integration)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-05 — Example Artifact`
- **Layout & Composition**: UI card preview of COSA Deal Pricing Experiment tracker.
- **Header Badge**: `COSA IMPLEMENTATION`
- **Main Headline**: **Tracking Pricing Elasticity in COSA Sales CRM**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Recording quoted prices, buyer reactions, and discount requests.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Quote Ledger: Log quoted contract values, billing cadence (Monthly vs. Annual), and proposal terms.
  - Objection Tagging: Tag pricing objections as #NoBudget, #ROI_Unclear, or #CompetitorCheaper.
  - Win-Rate by Price: COSA calculates your close rate at each pricing tier to reveal the revenue-maximizing price point.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: DATA GOVERNANCE: Never discount price without removing a feature. Protect your pricing integrity.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Mockup of COSA Sales CRM win-rate chart comparing deal conversion across $100, $300, and $500 price points.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Modern UI chart view on dark navy #070C18, bar graph comparing close rates across pricing tiers with glowing gold highlights.*

### Slide 5: Anti-Patterns vs. Best Practices (Comparative Matrix)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-06 — Decision Checkpoint`
- **Layout & Composition**: Side-by-side comparison table.
- **Header Badge**: `PRICING TRAPS`
- **Main Headline**: **Cost-Plus Pricing vs. Value-Based Pricing**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Why charging based on your server costs leaves 90% of your revenue on the table.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Cost-Plus Trap: 'Our server costs $10/user, so we will charge $15/user.' (Guarantees tiny profit margins).
  - Value-Based Mastery: If you save the customer $50,000 in lost payroll, charging $5,000/year is a 10x ROI for them.
  - Best Practice: Price against the *value created* or the *cost of the workaround replaced*, not your AWS bill.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: DECISION CHECKPOINT: Price at 10% to 20% of the quantifiable value or cost savings your solution creates.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Table comparing cost-plus margins with value-based pricing multipliers.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Comparison table on dark canvas: red hazard badges next to cost-plus calculations; teal checkmarks next to 10x ROI value pricing.*
