**Tone**: *Instructional, structured.*

> **Spoken Script**:

> "Master the five core formulas. [pause 0.5s] **ARPU** is your average revenue per user. **COGS** is your direct cost to serve them—including your server hosting and third-party AI API calls."
>
> "**Gross Margin** is the percentage left over after COGS; software businesses should aim for at least seventy-five to eighty percent. [pause 0.5s] **CAC** is your total cost to acquire one buyer. And **LTV** is the total gross profit that customer pays over their entire lifespan. Aim for an LTV to CAC ratio of at least three to one."
>
