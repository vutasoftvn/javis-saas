### Slide 2: The 5 Clauses of a Metric Contract (Contract Specification)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-04 — Focus Framework`
- **Layout & Composition**: 5-card vertical container stack on surface #0D172A.
- **Header Badge**: `CONTRACT SPECIFICATION`
- **Main Headline**: **The 5 Non-Negotiable Clauses of a Metric Contract**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Every core business metric must have these five attributes formally registered.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Clause 1: Canonical Name & Definition — What exact economic event does this metric represent? (e.g., Monthly Recurring Revenue).
  - Clause 2: Mathematical Formula — Explicit calculation logic: `Active Subscriptions × Contract Monthly Value` (excluding one-time setup fees).
  - Clause 3: Single Source of Truth — The exact database table or Stripe webhook event that powers the metric (no manual spreadsheets).
  - Clause 4: Human Owner — The single individual accountable for data integrity and review cadence.
  - Clause 5: Review Rhythm & Decision Threshold — Reviewed every Monday; alerts triggered if metric dips below target threshold.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: GOVERNANCE STANDARD: Setup fees and custom consulting can NEVER be counted inside Monthly Recurring Revenue.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Five stacked cards with icons for Definition, Formula, Database, Owner Silhouette, and Bell/Alert.

- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-02 — Definition Contrast`
- **Layout & Composition**: Side-by-side comparative table: Vanity Metric vs. Decision Metric.
- **Header Badge**: `SIGNAL INTEGRITY`
- **Main Headline**: **Vanity Theater vs. Commercial Reality**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Why top-of-funnel vanity metrics deceive founders into false confidence.
- **Core Slide Content**:
