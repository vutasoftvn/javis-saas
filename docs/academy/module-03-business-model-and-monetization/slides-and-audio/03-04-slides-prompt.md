### Slide 2: The 5 Core Unit Economic Metrics (Taxonomy & Formula)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-04 — Focus Framework`
- **Layout & Composition**: 5-card container layout on surface #0D172A.
- **Header Badge**: `METRIC EQUATIONS`
- **Main Headline**: **The 5 Essential Unit Economic Formulas**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Deconstructing the mathematics of venture sustainability.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - 1. Average Revenue Per User (ARPU): Total Revenue / Active Customer Count.
  - 2. Cost of Goods Sold (COGS): Direct costs to serve 1 customer (server hosting, third-party APIs, customer support).
  - 3. Gross Margin %: `(Revenue - COGS) / Revenue`. (Target for Software: >75-80%).
  - 4. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC): Total Sales & Marketing Spend / Number of New Customers Acquired.
  - 5. Customer Lifetime Value (LTV): `(ARPU × Gross Margin %) / Monthly Churn Rate`.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: THE GOLDEN RATIO: LTV to CAC must be greater than 3:1. CAC Payback period must be under 12 months.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Five horizontal equation cards with glowing mathematical symbols and benchmark thresholds.
