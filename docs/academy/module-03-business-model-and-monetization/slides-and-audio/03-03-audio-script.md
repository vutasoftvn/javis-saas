## SLIDE-SYNCHRONIZED AUDIO SCRIPT

### [SLIDE 1 AUDIO] — Title & Core Thesis (25s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 1 (Slide 1: Glowing gold pricing dial ascending from $49 to $499 with expanding value aura.)
**Tone**: *Commercial, bold, empowering.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "Pricing is the most powerful growth lever in your company, but it is almost always the most neglected. [pause 0.5s] First-time founders are so terrified of customer rejection that they price their product far too low."
>
> "Underpricing does not make sales easier; it makes sales harder. [pause 0.5s] When you charge pennies, buyers assume your software is fragile and unsecure. In Lesson 3.3, you will learn to run **Pricing Tests** to discover your true economic ceiling."
>

### [SLIDE 2 AUDIO] — The Van Westendorp Price Sensitivity Model (30s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 2 (Slide 2: Four stacked cards showing Too Cheap, Bargain, Expensive, and Too Expensive.)
**Tone**: *Instructional, scientific.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "Use the Van Westendorp pricing questions to map customer psychology. [pause 0.5s] Ask them: at what price does this feel so cheap that you doubt its security? At what price does it feel like an absolute bargain?"
>
> "At what price does it feel expensive, but you would still buy it? [pause 0.5s] And at what price is it completely out of the question? Your optimal price point is right below the 'too expensive' threshold. That is where maximum revenue and margin live."
>

### [SLIDE 3 AUDIO] — The 3 Practical Early-Stage Pricing Tests (25s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 3 (Slide 3: Three cards showing escalating price tags $200, $400, and $800.)
**Tone**: *Practical, strategic.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "Run an escalating price test. [pause 0.5s] Quote two hundred dollars a month to your first prospective buyer. If they agree without hesitation, quote four hundred dollars to your second buyer."
>
> "And quote eight hundred dollars to your third. [pause 0.5s] Keep doubling your price until thirty percent of qualified buyers push back. That pushback tells you that you have finally found the edge of your value proposition."
>

### [SLIDE 4 AUDIO] — Pricing Experiments in COSA Sales CRM (25s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 4 (Slide 4: COSA Sales CRM win-rate chart comparing conversion across price points.)
**Tone**: *Technical, architectural.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "In COSA Sales CRM, you track pricing elasticity across all your deals. [pause 0.5s] You log quoted prices, discount requests, and objection tags."
>
> "COSA calculates your win-rate at each price point. [pause 0.5s] Often, founders discover that their close rate is identical at two hundred dollars and five hundred dollars. That simple realization instantly more than doubles company revenue."
>

### [SLIDE 5 AUDIO] — Anti-Patterns vs. Best Practices (25s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 5 (Slide 5: Contrast table showing cost-plus calculations versus value-based ROI pricing.)
**Tone**: *Cautionary, coaching.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "Never price based on your costs. [pause 0.5s] The fact that your database hosting costs ten dollars a month is completely irrelevant to the customer."
>
> "Price based on the **value you create**. [pause 0.5s] If your software prevents a company from losing fifty thousand dollars in manual errors, charging five thousand dollars a year is a no-brainer ten-to-one return on investment for them. Price against their savings, not your costs."
>

### [SLIDE 6 AUDIO] — Founder Action Checkpoint (25s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 6 (Slide 6: Pricing test plan card showing Low, Target, and Stretch tiers.)
**Tone**: *Action-oriented, closing.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "Here is your deliverable for Lesson 3.3. [pause 0.5s] Open COSA Strategy and define your Low, Target, and Stretch pricing tiers."
>
> "Quote your Target price to your next three sales conversations, and record their reactions in Sales CRM. [pause 0.5s] In Lesson 3.4, we will take that pricing data and model your fundamental Unit Economics."
>
