### Slide 1: Title & Core Thesis (Hero Presentation)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-01 — Takeaway Claim`
- **Layout & Composition**: Hero presentation on #070C18 with glowing credit card and lockbox graphic.
- **Header Badge**: `COSA ACADEMY · MODULE 03 · LESSON 3.2`

- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Stated interest is not commercial intent; proving that real buyers will allocate budget and sign contracts.
- **Core Slide Content**:

  - Testing WTP requires structured buyer conversations that surface purchasing authority and budget friction.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: WTP AXIOM: A customer has not validated your pricing until money leaves their bank account or a binding contract is signed.

### Slide 2: The 4 Levels of Commercial Commitment (Commitment Taxonomy)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-04 — Focus Framework`
- **Layout & Composition**: 4-tier vertical pyramid on surface #0D172A.
- **Header Badge**: `COMMITMENT TIERS`
- **Main Headline**: **The Hierarchy of Buying Intent**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Separating superficial interest from genuine economic friction.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Tier 1 (Zero Commitment): Polite Compliment — 'We would love to use this.' (95% churn rate).
  - Tier 2 (Time & Data Commitment): Security Review / Data Sample — 'Here is our sanitized invoice dataset to test.'
  - Tier 3 (Legal Commitment): Letter of Intent (LOI) — Signed document agreeing to purchase if specific criteria are met.
  - Tier 4 (Financial Commitment): Pre-Order Deposit or Paid Pilot Agreement — Actual currency exchanged upfront.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: DISCOVERY RULE: Never declare business model validation based on Tier 1 compliments. Demand Tier 3 or Tier 4.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Four-tier pyramid diagram transitioning from muted grey base to glowing gold apex.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Tiered pyramid on dark navy canvas, four horizontal levels with glowing gold apex representing financial transactions.*

### Slide 3: The Economic Buyer vs. End User (Organizational Architecture)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-02 — Definition Contrast`
- **Layout & Composition**: Split comparative card layout: End User vs. Economic Buyer.
- **Header Badge**: `BUYER ARCHITECTURE`
- **Main Headline**: **End User Delight vs. Economic Buyer Authority**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Why loving the software doesn't mean having the corporate credit card.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - The End User (The Sufferer): Experiences the daily headache, uses the prototype, loves the UX. (Power: Zero budget authority).
  - The Economic Buyer (The Check-Signer): Manages the P&L, cares about ROI, risk, and headcount savings. (Power: Holds discretionary budget).
  - The Bridge: You must equip the End User with an internal ROI business case to convince their Economic Buyer.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: B2B REALITY: You sell to the Economic Buyer through the enthusiasm of the End User.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Split visual: Left side shows operations clerk with heart icon; right side shows CFO with wallet and key icon.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Two column contrast visual on dark navy: Left shows user silhouette with glowing cyan heart; right shows executive silhouette with glowing gold key.*

### Slide 4: WTP Tracking in COSA Sales CRM (COSA Workspace Integration)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-05 — Example Artifact`
- **Layout & Composition**: UI card preview of COSA Sales CRM Deal Card with Budget Verification tag.
- **Header Badge**: `COSA IMPLEMENTATION`
- **Main Headline**: **Tracking Willingness to Pay in COSA Sales CRM**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Recording buyer qualification, budget thresholds, and commitment tiers.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Deal Card Setup: Create opportunity records for each buyer interviewed in P1.
  - Budget Field: Record confirmed budget amount and discretionary spending limits.
  - Commitment Stage Tag: Tag deals as #Exploratory, #LOI_Signed, or #Deposit_Collected.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: SYSTEM PROTOCOL: A deal cannot advance past 'Qualification' without documenting the Economic Buyer's name and spending limit.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Mockup of COSA Sales CRM pipeline view with highlighted 'Budget Verified' badge on deal cards.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Modern CRM Kanban board preview on dark canvas #070C18, deal cards with glowing gold tags for verified budget numbers.*

### Slide 5: Anti-Patterns vs. Best Practices (Comparative Matrix)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-06 — Decision Checkpoint`
- **Layout & Composition**: Side-by-side comparison table.
- **Header Badge**: `WTP PITFALLS`
- **Main Headline**: **Fear of Asking for Money vs. Commercial Courage**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: How founders delay monetization validation out of fear of rejection.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Trap: Offering free pilots because asking for money feels uncomfortable or 'premature'.
  - Trap: Assuming that if they love the product, pricing discussions will magically take care of themselves.
  - Best Practice: Introduce pricing early in the conversation: 'Our production plans start at $500/month. Does that fit your team's budget?'
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: DECISION CHECKPOINT: Ask for the pre-order or deposit today. If they say no, you learn the truth while you still have runway.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Table comparing timid free giveaways with bold commercial pricing qualification.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Comparison table on dark canvas: red hazard badges next to free trial delays; teal checkmarks next to upfront pricing qualification.*

### Slide 6: Founder Action Checkpoint (Action Deliverable)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-07 — Learner Action`
- **Layout & Composition**: Action deliverable card container.
- **Header Badge**: `EXERCISE: WTP QUALIFICATION`
- **Main Headline**: **Conduct 3 Buyer Pricing Conversations in COSA**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Qualify economic buyers and test your baseline revenue hypothesis.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Step 1: Open COSA Sales CRM and create 3 prospect deals from your P1 testing cohort.
  - Step 2: Identify the Economic Buyer (the person with budget authority) for each company.
  - Step 3: Ask the anchor pricing question: 'We plan to price this at $X/month. What is your process for approving an expense of that size?'
  - Step 4: Record confirmed budget tiers in your COSA Deal Cards.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: DELIVERABLE: Document at least 2 verified budget confirmations in COSA Sales CRM before launching pricing tests.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Interactive card preview showing buyer qualification checklist with glowing gold status pills.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Clean modern UI card on dark navy #070C18, showing budget qualification fields with glowing checkmarks.*
