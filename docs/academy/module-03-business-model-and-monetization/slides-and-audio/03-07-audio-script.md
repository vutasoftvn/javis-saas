**Tone**: *Instructional, structured.*

> **Spoken Script**:

[pause 0.5s] Fourth, **Partnerships**: piggybacking on platforms like Shopify or Salesforce. And fifth, **Paid Ads**. Master outbound first before trying to automate."

### [SLIDE 3 AUDIO] — Channel-to-Price Fit (The ARPU-CAC Alignment) (25s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 3 (Slide 3: Spectrum mapping Fly, Rabbit, Deer, and Elephant along price curve.)
**Tone**: *Analytical, precise.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "Your price point dictates your allowable acquisition channel. [pause 0.5s] If you sell a ten-dollar-a-month tool to consumers, you cannot hire human sales reps to close deals; you will go bankrupt on day one. You must rely on organic virality or SEO."
>
> "Conversely, if you sell a fifty-thousand-dollar enterprise software contract, you cannot expect buyers to click a self-serve checkout button. [pause 0.5s] High contract values require human relationship selling; low contract values require zero-touch automation."
>
