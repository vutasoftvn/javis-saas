- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-01 — Takeaway Claim`
- **Layout & Composition**: Hero layout with glowing distribution pipeline graphic on #070C18 dark canvas.
- **Header Badge**: `COSA ACADEMY · MODULE 03 · LESSON 3.7`
- **Main Headline**: **Optimizing Sales Channels: Distribution Velocity**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Poor distribution—not poor product—is the number one cause of startup failure; mastering the economics of acquisition.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Peter Thiel's Law: 'Superior sales and distribution by itself can create a monopoly, even with no product differentiation.'
  - A sales channel is not just where you find customers; it determines your pricing, your product complexity, and your CAC.
  - The early objective is not scaling 5 channels simultaneously, but proving ONE repeatable channel that generates positive unit economics.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: DISTRIBUTION LAW: Most startups have zero distribution channels that work. If you can get even ONE channel to work predictably, you have a great business.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Visual pipeline: An illuminated conduit branching into five pathways, with a single central glowing teal pipeline flowing with high velocity.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Stylized pipeline graphic on dark slate #070C18: five branching conduits, with one central neon teal pipeline glowing intensely and delivering steady energy nodes.*

### Slide 2: The 5 Go-To-Market Channel Archetypes (Taxonomy Matrix)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-04 — Focus Framework`
- **Layout & Composition**: 5-card container layout on surface #0D172A.
- **Header Badge**: `CHANNEL TAXONOMY`
- **Main Headline**: **The 5 Primary Distribution Channels**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Matching your price point and customer behavior to the appropriate channel motion.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - 1. Founder-Led Direct Outbound: Personalized cold email, LinkedIn messaging, and direct calls. Essential for first 20 B2B deals.
  - 2. Inbound / Content & SEO: Thought leadership, technical blogs, and guides that attract searching buyers over time. High upfront effort, low marginal cost.
  - 3. Product-Led Growth (PLG): Self-serve freemium or viral collaboration features that spread organically through the user's workflow.
  - 4. Ecosystem & Platform Partnerships: App stores, integration marketplaces (Shopify, Salesforce, Slack), and agency partner referrals.
  - 5. Paid Performance Acquisition: Google Search Ads, LinkedIn sponsored content, and targeted social ads. Requires proven LTV:CAC >3x.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: EARLY MANDATE: Every founder must start with Channel 1 (Founder-Led Outbound) before attempting automated channels.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Five vertical cards with distinct icon badges: Phone (Outbound), Megaphone (Inbound), Loop (PLG), Puzzle (Partnerships), and Target (Paid).
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Five sleek glass cards on dark background, glowing teal and gold icon badges, clean typography.*

### Slide 3: Channel-to-Price Fit (The ARPU-CAC Alignment) (Economic Matrix)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-02 — Definition Contrast`
- **Layout & Composition**: Comparative alignment spectrum: Hunting Flies, Rabbits, Deer, and Elephants.
- **Header Badge**: `CHANNEL FIT SPECTRUM`
- **Main Headline**: **The Product-Channel Fit Spectrum**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Why your Annual Contract Value (ACV) dictates your allowable acquisition channel.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Mice / Flies (<$100/yr): Must use virality or organic SEO. High-touch human sales will bankrupt you immediately.
  - Rabbits ($1k - $5k/yr): Supports Product-Led Growth (PLG), inside sales demos, and targeted paid performance ads.
  - Deer ($10k - $50k/yr): Supports dedicated SDR/AE outbound sales reps, customized proposals, and webinars.
  - Elephants ($100k+/yr): Requires high-touch enterprise field sales, on-site security reviews, executive dinners, and pilots.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: ALIGNMENT RULE: You cannot use enterprise sales reps to sell a $50/month tool, and you cannot sell a $100k contract with a self-serve checkout button.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Spectrum diagram mapping animal icons (Fly, Rabbit, Deer, Elephant) along an ascending price and CAC curve.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Horizontal spectrum graphic on dark canvas #070C18: ascending price curve mapping glowing icons for Fly, Rabbit, Deer, and Elephant.*

- **Highlight / Accent Box**: DATA DISCIPLINE: Stop guessing where your best leads come from. Require channel attribution on every deal record.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Mockup of COSA Channel Velocity view with comparative conversion funnels and CAC gauges.

### Slide 5: Anti-Patterns vs. Best Practices (Comparative Matrix)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-06 — Decision Checkpoint`
- **Layout & Composition**: Side-by-side comparison table.
- **Header Badge**: `DISTRIBUTION PITFALLS`
- **Main Headline**: **Channel Sprawl vs. Singular Mastery**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: How attempting to do every marketing channel at once guarantees failure.

  - Trap: Abandoning an outbound channel after sending 15 emails because 'it didn't work'.
