# Module 1 — Seedance Production Pack

## Production decision

Module 1 has **nine lessons**, each assembled from **three complete 28-second Seedance videos**. Each render is a finished scene with synchronized English speech, music, sound design, founder, and—when helpful—one in-frame teaching slide. Do not generate detached founder footage, detached slide footage, product walkthroughs, or fake COSA interfaces.

The three-video learning rhythm remains constant:

1. **Hook:** founder focus, no slide; founder fills 65–70% of frame.
2. **Research model:** founder at left 38%, teaching screen at right 62%.
3. **Practice:** founder at left 38%, action or example screen at right 62%, ending on a concrete learner action.

Each lesson is approximately **84 seconds** after joining its three clips with a 0.4–0.6-second soft dissolve or teal-light sweep.

## Continuity with Module 0

Use the same recurring host from Module 0: a Vietnamese male founder and operating coach around 40, short dark hair, dark smart-casual overshirt over a neutral shirt, polished editorial illustration, warm and credible. Reuse an approved still from Module 0 Video 01 as the Seedance character reference when possible. Never change face, hairstyle, wardrobe, voice, or visual style.

Module 1 keeps the same COSA dark-navy studio but adds a quiet **research studio** texture: distant abstract paper notes, pale quote marks, a soft teal thread map, and warm desk light. These are background atmosphere only—not a dashboard, whiteboard app, or product UI.

## Audio and edit language

Every prompt specifies the full sound treatment:

- Native-level English male voice, about 40, warm, measured, neutral international accent, exact spoken script and natural lip sync.
- Minimal warm electronic ambient bed at 78 BPM; no vocal, no heavy beat; voice clearly leads the mix.
- Only soft light-whooshes for slide appearance, gentle tonal lifts for insight, and a restrained resolve tone for learner action.
- No typing, notification pings, crowd, applause, cinematic impacts, English narration, invented dialogue, or unrelated effects.
- Preserve a 0.75-second visual handle at each end. Keep the lower 18% clear for English captions added in post.

## Teaching screens

Use the COSA-derived dark canvas, navy glow, teal, light teal, sky blue, rose warning, white, and muted slate system documented in Module 0’s [in-frame slide system](../../module-00-founder-foundations/production/slide-system.md). Screens hold one takeaway title and one simple research model or example. They never resemble the real COSA UI.
