### [SLIDE 2 AUDIO] — Context vs. Demographics (30s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 2 (Slide 2: Split comparison between generic demographic avatar and contextual workflow.)
**Tone**: *Analytical, instructional.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "Stop defining customers by useless demographic labels. [pause 0.5s] Knowing that someone is a 35-year-old manager at a mid-sized company tells you virtually nothing about whether they will buy your software."
>
> "Define them by **operational context**. [pause 0.5s] What tools are open on their screen right now? What exact workflow makes them want to pull their hair out every Tuesday at 2 PM? Customers buy software to fix broken situations, not demographic traits."
>

### [SLIDE 3 AUDIO] — The 4 Beachhead Evaluation Filters (30s)
**Slide Reference**: Slide 3 (Slide 3: 2x2 grid showing Urgency, Budget, Channels, and References.)
**Tone**: *Rigorous, diagnostic.*

> **Spoken Script**:
>
> "How do you know if you picked the right beachhead? Put your candidate segment through four filters. [pause 0.5s] First, urgency: is the problem threatening their business today? Second, budget: do they have the authority to swipe a card without asking three vice presidents?"
>
> "Third, access: can you easily find and message one hundred of them? [pause 0.5s] And fourth, community: do they talk to each other? If they are connected, one happy customer will bring you five more."
>
