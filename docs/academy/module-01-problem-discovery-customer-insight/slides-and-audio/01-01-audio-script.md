Welcome to Module 01: Problem Discovery and Customer Insight. [pause 0.5s] The most tragic mistake in the startup world is building a technically brilliant solution that nobody actually wants or needs.

In Stage P0 Discovery, we do not build software. We act as detectives. [pause 0.5s] Our sole objective is to prove that a specific group of customers suffers from an acute, urgent, and expensive problem.

A vague problem cannot be solved. [pause 0.5s] In COSA, every valid problem must have five distinct pillars. Who is the exact customer? What is the specific trigger context when the breakdown happens?

What job are they trying to complete? What clumsy, frustrating workaround do they use today? [pause 0.5s] And what is the measurable consequence—in wasted hours, lost money, or compliance penalties—when that workaround fails?

Beware of the solution-first trap. [pause 0.5s] When a founder has a clever technology idea, they immediately rush to pitch it. They ask people: 'Wouldn't it be great if an AI did this for you?'

People are polite; they will smile and say yes. [pause 0.5s] But polite compliments are not customer validation. Never mention your product idea during discovery. Focus 100% on understanding their painful reality.

In COSA, you document these insights in your Problem Records. [pause 0.5s] You don't keep rough thoughts in your head or in scattered text files.

You record the customer persona, attach direct quotes, link interview recordings in the Vault, and assign a confidence rating. [pause 0.5s] This creates an audit trail of real evidence for your venture.

Here is the golden rule of discovery: **past behavior predicts future action; stated intent predicts nothing**. [pause 0.5s] Never ask someone what they would do in the future.

Ask them about the last time they experienced the problem. [pause 0.5s] If they cannot remember the last time it happened, or if they haven't spent a single dollar or hour trying to solve it, the problem is not painful enough.

Here is your deliverable for Lesson 1.1. [pause 0.5s] Open your COSA Strategy workspace and write out your five-part Problem Statement.

Identify your target customer, the friction, the trigger, the workaround, and the quantified cost. [pause 0.5s] Lock this in, and in our next lesson, we will learn how to conduct qualitative discovery interviews to test it.
