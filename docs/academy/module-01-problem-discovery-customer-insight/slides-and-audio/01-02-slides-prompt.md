## SLIDE-BY-SLIDE SPECIFICATIONS

### Slide 1: Title & Core Thesis (Hero Presentation)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-01 — Takeaway Claim`
- **Layout & Composition**: Hero presentation on #070C18 dark canvas with glowing audio waveform motif.
- **Header Badge**: `COSA ACADEMY · MODULE 01 · LESSON 1.2`
- **Main Headline**: **Conducting Qualitative Discovery Interviews**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: How to extract unbiased behavioral evidence from prospective customers without pitching, biasing, or leading.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - The goal of a discovery interview is not to sell your vision; it is to understand the customer's operational reality.
  - Bad interviews collect polite confirmation; great interviews uncover hidden workarounds, frustrations, and budgets.
  - Applying 'The Mom Test' principles ensures every conversation yields objective commercial intelligence.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: THE INTERVIEW LAW: Listen 80% of the time, speak 20%. If you are talking about your product, you are failing.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Visual waveform: An audio waveform on dark canvas transitioning from chaotic red chatter into a focused, glowing teal insight pulse.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Stylized graphic of glowing sound waves on deep navy background #070C18, transforming into clean geometric data nodes.*

### Slide 2: The 5 Non-Leading Question Archetypes (Framework & Model)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-04 — Focus Framework`
- **Layout & Composition**: 5-card vertical accordion on surface #0D172A.
- **Header Badge**: `QUESTION ARSENAL`
- **Main Headline**: **The 5 Golden Discovery Questions**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Open-ended questions anchored strictly in past reality.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - 1. Context Trigger: 'Walk me through the last time [Problem X] occurred in your workflow.'
  - 2. Pain Anchor: 'What was the hardest or most frustrating part about that situation?'
  - 3. Current Workaround: 'What tools, spreadsheets, or manual hacks did you use to handle it?'
  - 4. Cost & Consequence: 'What did that breakdown cost your team in time, revenue, or customer goodwill?'
  - 5. Solution Search: 'What other software or services have you evaluated or purchased to fix this?'
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: PRO TIP: Always follow up with: 'Why was that a problem?' and 'Can you show me your screen?'
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Five clean container cards with glowing cyan question marks and formatted question scripts.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Five stacked minimalist card modules on dark slate background, glowing cyan number badges and crisp typography.*

### Slide 3: The Anatomy of an Interview Guide (Process & Architecture)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-05 — Example Artifact`
- **Layout & Composition**: Three-phase timeline layout: Kickoff (5m), Deep Dive (20m), Debrief (5m).
- **Header Badge**: `INTERVIEW STRUCTURE`
- **Main Headline**: **Structuring a 30-Minute Discovery Session**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: A disciplined timebox that respects customer time and maximizes signal.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Phase 1: Rapport & Context (5 mins) — Set psychological safety: 'I am researching how companies handle logistics; I have nothing to sell.'
  - Phase 2: Historical Deep-Dive (20 mins) — Drill down into specific past episodes, screen shares, and daily friction.
  - Phase 3: Referral & Permission (5 mins) — 'Who else on your team deals with this? Can I follow up as my research progresses?'
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: CRITICAL PROTOCOL: Never ask for permission to pitch. Ask for introductions to other sufferers.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: 3-stage horizontal timeline bar showing 5m, 20m, and 5m phase allocations with icon markers.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Horizontal timeline on dark canvas #070C18, three glowing time segments in teal and sky blue, clean modern markers.*

### Slide 4: Recording Signals in COSA Vault (COSA Workspace Integration)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-05 — Example Artifact`
- **Layout & Composition**: Card preview showing structured interview note template in COSA Vault.
- **Header Badge**: `EVIDENCE CAPTURE`
- **Main Headline**: **Capturing High-Fidelity Notes in COSA**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Separating direct quotes from founder interpretations.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Raw Transcripts: Upload audio recordings to Vault for automated AI transcription.
  - Quote Tagging: Highlight verbatim customer words describing pain, money, and time.
  - Observation vs. Interpretation: Keep what the customer *actually said* separate from what you *think it means*.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: INTEGRITY RULE: If it's not a direct quote, mark it as an unproven founder assumption.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Split document view: Left side shows verbatim customer quote in cyan; right side shows metadata tag.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Digital document preview on dark navy, glowing quotation marks, structured tags for Severity, Frequency, and Workaround.*

### Slide 5: Anti-Patterns vs. Best Practices (Comparative Matrix)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-06 — Decision Checkpoint`
- **Layout & Composition**: Side-by-side comparison table.
- **Header Badge**: `INTERVIEW PITFALLS`
- **Main Headline**: **Leading the Witness vs. Uncovering Truth**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Recognizing biased interview techniques that produce false positives.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Trap: 'Don't you think it would be better if software automated this?' (Leading question).
  - Trap: Explaining why your proposed feature will solve their complaint when they object.
  - Best Practice: Embrace awkward pauses; let the customer fill the silence with honest reflections.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: DECISION CHECKPOINT: When a customer says 'That wouldn't work for us,' thank them warmly and dig deeper.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Comparison table contrasting biased leading questions with neutral objective prompts.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Table layout on dark canvas: red hazard badges next to pitch-mode habits; teal checkmarks next to detective-mode habits.*
