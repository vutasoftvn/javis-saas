### Slide 2: The 3 Dimensions Deconstructed (Framework & Model)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-04 — Focus Framework`
- **Layout & Composition**: 3-column container comparison on surface #0D172A.
- **Header Badge**: `THE 3 DIMENSIONS`
- **Main Headline**: **Functional, Emotional, and Social Jobs**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Why functional features alone rarely close the sale.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - 1. Functional Job: The tangible task to accomplish (e.g., 'Reconcile 50 invoices before the monthly accounting close').
  - 2. Emotional Job: How the customer wants to feel or avoid feeling (e.g., 'Feel confident that I won't get audited or look incompetent').
  - 3. Social Job: How the customer wants to be perceived by peers, bosses, or clients (e.g., 'Be seen as a modern, data-driven finance director').
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: CONVERSION SECRET: Functional jobs get you on the shortlist; emotional and social jobs make them sign the contract.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Three distinct vertical cards with color-coded thematic headers: Teal (Functional), Gold (Emotional), Purple (Social).
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Three sleek glass cards on dark background, distinct icon headers: gear for functional, heart/shield for emotional, crowd/pedestal for social.*

### Slide 3: The Forces of Progress (Behavioral Dynamics)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-03 — Operating Loop`
- **Layout & Composition**: 4-quadrant push/pull vector diagram.
- **Header Badge**: `BEHAVIORAL FORCES`
- **Main Headline**: **The 4 Forces That Drive or Block Adoption**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Analyzing the psychological tug-of-war behind every customer purchase.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Force 1: Push of Current Pain — 'Our current process is so slow and error-prone that we can't continue like this.'
  - Force 2: Pull of the New Solution — 'If we switch to this new tool, we will save 10 hours a week and look like heroes.'
  - Force 3: Anxiety of the Unknown — 'What if implementation takes months? What if data gets corrupted?' (Blocks purchase).
  - Force 4: Habit & Inertia — 'We are used to our messy spreadsheets, and everyone knows how they work.' (Blocks purchase).
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: ADOPTION EQUATION: (Push + Pull) must be significantly greater than (Anxiety + Habit).
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Vector physics diagram: Two forward green arrows (Push & Pull) pushing against two backward red resistance arrows (Anxiety & Habit).
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Dynamic vector graphic on dark canvas: Bright teal and cyan force arrows overcoming opposing glowing crimson resistance arrows.*
