## SLIDE-BY-SLIDE SPECIFICATIONS

### Slide 1: Title & Core Thesis (Hero Presentation)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-01 — Takeaway Claim`
- **Layout & Composition**: Hero layout with radar signal filter on #070C18 dark canvas.
- **Header Badge**: `COSA ACADEMY · MODULE 01 · LESSON 1.5`
- **Main Headline**: **Distinguishing Real Problem Signals from Assumptions**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: How founders separate high-fidelity behavioral evidence from polite opinions and wishful assumptions.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Founders are naturally optimistic and prone to confirmation bias, treating polite smiles as product validation.
  - Real signals leave an empirical audit trail: lost revenue, angry client escalations, expensive custom workarounds, and late nights.
  - Assumptions are unproven beliefs that masquerade as facts until subjected to falsifiable testing.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: THE SIGNAL RULE: Opinion is cheap; historical behavior is expensive. Always bet on what they did, not what they say.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Visual spectrum filter: A noisy spectrum of grey opinions passing through a digital filter, outputting only sharp glowing cyan evidence nodes.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Minimalist digital signal processing graphic on dark slate #070C18: diffuse red/grey noise waves passing through a neon teal filter into clean square data points.*

### Slide 2: The 4-Tier Evidence Taxonomy (Taxonomy & Hierarchy)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-02 — Definition Contrast`
- **Layout & Composition**: 4-tier vertical container stack from Weakest to Strongest.
- **Header Badge**: `SIGNAL FIDELITY`
- **Main Headline**: **The 4 Classes of Research Statements**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Classifying every customer research note into its true evidentiary grade.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Grade D (Opinion / Speculation): 'I think companies in our space will need this soon.' (Value: Zero).
  - Grade C (Stated Intent): 'If you launch next month, I would definitely subscribe.' (Value: Low / False positive).
  - Grade B (Documented Past Behavior): 'Last Thursday, we had to refund $4,000 to a client because our inventory sync failed.' (Value: High).
  - Grade A (Active Capital / Resource Commitment): 'Here is our API key and our credit card; please automate this for us today.' (Value: Irrefutable).
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: DISCOVERY DISCIPLINE: Never base a product development roadmap on Grade C or Grade D statements.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Four horizontal cards stacked vertically with color badges: Grey (D), Rose (C), Sky Blue (B), Vibrant Teal (A).
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Four stacked horizontal data cards on deep navy canvas, progressive color coding from muted slate to glowing neon teal.*

### Slide 3: The Confirmation Bias Audit (Diagnostic Framework)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-06 — Decision Checkpoint`
- **Layout & Composition**: Two-column comparison: Founder Assumption vs. Customer Evidence.
- **Header Badge**: `BIAS DIAGNOSTIC`
- **Main Headline**: **The Cognitive Bias Audit**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: How to catch yourself reinterpreting weak customer reactions to fit your preconceived solution.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Trap 1: The 'Loud Participant' Fallacy — Changing your entire product spec because one passionate person complained.
  - Trap 2: Explaining Away Objections — 'They only rejected it because they don't understand modern AI workflows.'
  - The Scientific Antidote: Actively search for disconfirming evidence. Try to prove your hypothesis wrong.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: SCIENTIFIC RIGOR: If you cannot find anyone who actively rejects your problem statement, your interviews are too superficial.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Split visual: A warped subjective mirror on the left versus an objective forensic scale on the right.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Symbolic visual on dark navy: Left shows a warped red funhouse mirror reflecting a distorted idea; right shows a glowing teal balanced scientific scale.*

### Slide 4: Tagging Notes in COSA Vault (COSA Workspace Integration)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-05 — Example Artifact`
- **Layout & Composition**: UI card preview of COSA Vault Evidence Tagger.
- **Header Badge**: `COSA IMPLEMENTATION`
- **Main Headline**: **Evidence Classification in COSA Vault**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Tagging customer insights as Observation, Quote, Assumption, or Evidence Gap.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Tag: #Observation — Verifiable actions witnessed during screen-shares or on-site visits.
  - Tag: #VerbatimQuote — Exact customer words describing pain, cost, or workarounds.
  - Tag: #Assumption — Any assertion made by the founder that lacks customer verification.
  - Tag: #EvidenceGap — Missing data that requires a targeted follow-up interview.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: SYSTEM INTEGRITY: COSA highlights unverified assumptions in yellow to ensure founders stay honest.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Mockup of COSA Vault note editor with colorful pill tags applied to individual text sentences.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Modern digital note card on dark background #070C18 with glowing inline pill tags for #Quote, #Observation, and #Assumption.*

### Slide 5: Anti-Patterns vs. Best Practices (Comparative Matrix)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-06 — Decision Checkpoint`
- **Layout & Composition**: Side-by-side comparison table.
- **Header Badge**: `EVIDENCE SANITY CHECKS`
- **Main Headline**: **Wishful Thinking vs. Empirical Discipline**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Safeguarding your venture from self-deception.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Trap: Counting LinkedIn likes or waitlist emails as problem validation.
  - Trap: Assuming that a problem is urgent just because it makes logical sense on paper.
  - Best Practice: Require at least 5 independent, unprompted accounts of active monetary or time loss.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: DECISION RULE: A waitlist email is curiosity. A calendar invite or raw log file is validation.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Table comparing superficial traction metrics with deep operational validation evidence.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Comparison table on dark canvas: red penalty badges for vanity email counts; teal verification checks for operational data.*

### Slide 6: Founder Action Checkpoint (Action Deliverable)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-07 — Learner Action`
- **Layout & Composition**: Action template card container.
- **Header Badge**: `EXERCISE: EVIDENCE AUDIT`
- **Main Headline**: **Audit Your Research Notes in COSA Vault**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Cleanse your research database of opinions and tag genuine evidence.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Step 1: Open your latest customer interview notes in COSA Vault.
  - Step 2: Apply the four tags (#Observation, #Quote, #Assumption, #EvidenceGap) to at least 10 statements.
  - Step 3: Count your ratio: If >50% of your notes are #Assumption, schedule 3 more discovery calls.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: DELIVERABLE: Convert all assumptions into testable hypotheses before advancing to Solution Fit.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Progress bar showing Evidence vs. Assumption ratio with glowing teal pass threshold.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Audit meter graphic on dark slate canvas: progress bar showing 70% Evidence ratio with bright teal success badge.*
