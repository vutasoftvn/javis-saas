- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-04 — Focus Framework`
- **Layout & Composition**: 3-tier vertical container stack on surface #0D172A.
- **Header Badge**: `OKR CASCADING`
- **Main Headline**: **The 3 Layers of Strategic Cascading**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Connecting long-term enterprise goals to frontline daily sprint work.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Level 1: The Annual Strategic Pillar (Executive Board) — The overarching corporate bet (e.g., 'Establish European Market Leadership').
  - Level 2: The 12-Week Team OKR (Pod Level) — Objective: Qualitative inspirational goal; Key Results: 2-3 measurable quantitative targets.
  - Level 3: The Weekly Tactical Sprint (Daily Execution) — High-priority tasks tagged to specific Key Results, tracked on Kanban boards.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: OKR PURITY: Key Results must always be numbers, percentages, or dollars. If a Key Result does not have a number, it is a task, not an OKR.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Three stacked cards with cascading connectors showing Annual Pillar, 12-Week OKR, and Weekly Sprint Task.

### Slide 3: Activity Busywork vs. Outcome-Driven OKRs (Measurement Contrast)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-02 — Definition Contrast`
- **Layout & Composition**: Two-panel split: Activity Focus vs. Outcome Focus.
- **Header Badge**: `MEASUREMENT INTEGRITY`
- **Main Headline**: **Output Activity Busywork vs. Measurable Business Outcomes**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Why measuring completed tasks produces false confidence and zero enterprise progress.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Activity Busywork (Flawed): 'Launch our new marketing blog; write 10 articles; redesign the dashboard.' (High effort, zero guaranteed value).
  - Measurable Outcome (COSA Standard): 'Increase organic demo requests from 15 to 45 per week with CAC <$400.' (Empirical business impact).
  - The Rule: It does not matter how many tickets your team completed this week if the business outcome failed to move.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: MANAGEMENT LAW: Never confuse effort with results. Reward teams for moving the needle on the Key Result, not for breaking a sweat.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Split visual: Left shows spinning hamster wheel with red sweat droplets; right shows precision arrow hitting gold bullseye.

### Slide 5: Anti-Patterns vs. Best Practices (Comparative Matrix)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-06 — Decision Checkpoint`

- **Header Badge**: `OKR TRAPS`

- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Avoiding the common planning mistakes that render OKRs useless.
