### Slide 2: The Cross-Functional Outcome Pod Architecture (Organizational Blueprint)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-04 — Focus Framework`
- **Layout & Composition**: 4-part container layout on surface #0D172A.
- **Header Badge**: `POD ARCHITECTURE`
- **Main Headline**: **Autonomous Cross-Functional Outcome Pods**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Deconstructing the self-sufficient unit of enterprise execution.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Pod Composition: 1 Product Lead, 2-3 Engineers, 1 Designer, 1 Growth/GTM Operator, and dedicated COSA AI agents.
  - Outcome Charter: Each pod owns a single business outcome (e.g., 'New User Activation' or 'Enterprise Expansion'), NOT a code layer.
  - Autonomous Authority: The pod has complete authority to ship experiments within its domain without executive permission.
  - Interface Contracts: Pods interact with other teams via strict APIs and data contracts, preventing dependency bottlenecks.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: THE TWO-PIZZA RULE: If a pod cannot be fed by two pizzas (5-7 people), it is too large and must be subdivided.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Four horizontal cards showing Pod Composition, Outcome Charter, Autonomous Authority, and Interface Contracts.
- **AI Visual Generation Directive**: *Four sleek glassmorphic cards in horizontal alignment on deep navy canvas, glowing cyan borders, clean minimalist team icons.*

### Slide 3: Functional Silos vs. Autonomous Outcome Pods (Structural Contrast)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-02 — Definition Contrast`
- **Layout & Composition**: Two-panel split: Rigid Hierarchical Silos vs. Modular Outcome Pods.
- **Header Badge**: `STRUCTURAL CONTRAST`
- **Main Headline**: **Functional Silo Gridlock vs. Autonomous Pod Velocity**
- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Why traditional corporate pyramids crush innovation as companies scale.
- **Core Slide Content**:
  - Functional Silos (Bureaucracy): Product writes a spec, hands off to Design, throws over wall to Engineering, waits for QA, begs Marketing. (Ship cycle: 4 months).
  - Autonomous Pods (Velocity): Product, Engineering, Design, and Marketing sit in one pod, align on the outcome, and ship daily. (Ship cycle: 48 hours).
  - The Operational Result: 10 small autonomous pods ship 5x faster than a 100-person functional department.
- **Highlight / Accent Box**: VELOCITY PRINCIPLE: Hand-offs between departments are where startup velocity goes to die. Eliminate departmental hand-offs.
- **Diagram / Visual Structure**: Split visual: Left shows rigid vertical gray silos with stuck conveyor belts; right shows glowing cyan modular pods firing rapid data pulses.

### Slide 5: Anti-Patterns vs. Best Practices (Comparative Matrix)
- **Visual Archetype**: `SL-06 — Decision Checkpoint`
- **Layout & Composition**: Side-by-side comparison table.
- **Header Badge**: `SCALING PITFALLS`

- **Sub-headline / Thesis**: Avoiding the accountability vacuum of matrixed reporting structures.

  - Trap: Letting pods become isolated kingdoms that duplicate infrastructure and build incompatible tools.
